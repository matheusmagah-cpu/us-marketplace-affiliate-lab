# Evidência de compra: criadores de currículo online no Brasil (vs EUA/global), out/2026

Research date: 2026-10-26. Method limitation: reclameaqui.com.br, play.google.com and rocket-resume.com returned DNS errors from this environment. Figures from those sites come from search-engine snippets of their pages and could not be re-checked by opening the page. Labels: **[VERIFICADO-snippet]** = figure seen in a search snippet of the primary page; **[TERCEIROS]** = aggregator, blog or estimate; **[INFERÊNCIA]** = my own calculation.

## 1. Criadores de currículo pagos no Brasil: preços e sinais de venda

### Takeaway
The paid BR market is dominated by the BOLD group (Zety BR, Meu Currículo Perfeito / MyPerfectResume BR, LiveCareer BR, and probably Online Currículo). They use the US playbook in BRL: a 14-day trial for R$4,99–8,70 that auto-renews at R$32,80–49,90 every 4 weeks. Meu Currículo Perfeito is the clearest sign of real paid volume in Brazil.

### Cited Findings
- **Meu Currículo Perfeito (BOLD):** 14-day trial at R$4,99 that, per the company's own reply on Reclame Aqui, auto-enrolls the user in a monthly subscription of R$39,99 renewed every 4 weeks. [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [Reclame Aqui – Online Currículo/MCP complaints](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/online-curriculo/cobranca-indevida-de-assinatura-nao-autorizada_QdJ6QYtPu9f416QJ/); [Reclame Aqui – MCP list](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/meu-curriculo-perfeito/lista-reclamacoes/)
- MyPerfectResume belongs to BOLD (the same group as Zety, LiveCareer, ResumeNow). The App Store listing is © 2026 BOLD Limited and includes an in-app item called "Meu Currículo Perfeito - teste". [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [App Store MyPerfectCV](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/myperfectcv-resume-cv-builder/id1576380854)
- Trustpilot shows meucurriculoperfeito.com.br at 4.8 stars with about 5 thousand reviews. That indicates large real usage, though BOLD's review solicitation likely inflates the score. [TERCEIROS] — [Trustpilot BR – resume service](https://br.trustpilot.com/categories/resume_service)
- **Zety Brasil:** a January 2024 offer of 14 days for R$8,70. The company's reply says the trial auto-enrolls the user in a subscription of BRL 49,70 every 4 weeks. 2025 complaints cite charges of R$49,70 under the descriptor "DM *ZETY BR". [VERIFICADO-snippet, Portal da Queixa/Reclame Aqui] — [Portal da Queixa – Zety](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/zety/complaints/zety-cobranca-indevida-e-sem-ordem-99744023); [Reclame Aqui – Zety](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/zety/cobranca-indevida-zety-criador-de-curriculo_H1-kSVD_YLA4acjL/)
- **LiveCareer BR:** a March 2026 complaint describes a R$5,95 trial followed by a R$32,80 charge. The company's reply confirms the model: R$5,95 for a 14-day trial, then a premium renewal. [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [Reclame Aqui – LiveCareer](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/livecareer/cobranca-indevida-apos-solicitacao-de-curriculo_7QuJ1AMi22uKH9PD/)

- **"Criar CV" (company name redacted):** R$3,45 to download a PDF, followed by a larger unauthorized debit. [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [Reclame Aqui – criar-cv](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/criar-cv/cobranca-indevida_eeI3s0i6GCLnc61U/)
- **Catho:** in Nov/2021 it made applying to jobs free for candidates (it used to charge about R$60/month after 30 free days) because of competition. In 2012 it charged R$249,20 for highlighting until the user was hired. — [Betterteam](https://www.betterteam.com/br/curriculum-com-br); [Baguete 2012](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/curriculum-r-249-ate-o-usuario-ser-contratado)
- **Kickresume** (pt version): free export without the premium features. The iOS app is available in the BR store. — [Kickresume pt](https://www.kickresume.com/pt/aplicativo-movel/play-store/); [App Store BR](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/kickresume-ai-resume-builder/id1523618695)

### Inferences
- BOLD runs at least 3–4 brands in pt-BR with dedicated BRL billing (the "DM *ZETY BR" descriptor). A multinational with paid acquisition would not localize billing and support for years unless the market paid back.
- The BR ticket in practice is R$5–9 upfront plus one or more renewals of R$33–50. The revenue per customer that sustains these companies comes largely from renewals that many customers did not want, which is the source of the complaints.

### Gaps
- I found no paid-user counts for any BR brand. I found no pricing page for CVwizard, Currículo Fácil, MeuCurrículo, Novorésumé pt, or resume.io pt-BR, and could not open those sites from here.
- No current price for Catho's premium highlight plan.

## 2. Reclame Aqui: contagens como prova de clientes pagantes

### Takeaway
The complaint volume is direct proof of paying customers: each complaint presupposes a card charge. Meu Currículo Perfeito alone received 671 complaints in 6 months (Apr–Sep 2026), about 110 per month. Zety BR received 75 in 12 months.

### Cited Findings
- **Meu Currículo Perfeito:** score 6,6/10, "Regular", period 01/04/2026–30/09/2026: 671 complaints, 100% answered, 73,5% resolved, 37% would do business again. Recent complaints include a Premium plan bought 21/09/2026 and charged after the trial. [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [Reclame Aqui – MCP](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/meu-curriculo-perfeito/)
- The same company's complaint-list page shows the category "Cobrança indevida (6787)". This is probably an all-time accumulated count; I could not confirm the window. [VERIFICADO-snippet, period uncertain] — [Reclame Aqui – MCP list](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/meu-curriculo-perfeito/lista-reclamacoes/)
- **Online Currículo:** category "Cobrança indevida" with 635 records (period not identified). [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [Reclame Aqui – Online Currículo list](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/online-curriculo/lista-reclamacoes/)
- **Zety:** 75 complaints between 01/10/2025 and 30/09/2026, 100% answered, 81,5% resolved, not verified, no RA1000 seal. Main problem: cobrança indevida (~75%). Cases include R$49,90 after 14 days and R$5,99/month charged for over a year. [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [Reclame Aqui – Zety](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/zety/)
- In 2024, a complaint about "propaganda enganosa" said the site advertised a free résumé and then charged before the download. [VERIFICADO-snippet] — [Reclame Aqui – MCP propaganda enganosa](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/meu-curriculo-perfeito/propaganda-enganosa_oqx64IEMAGeZ1fGF/)

### Inferences
- [INFERÊNCIA] If 0,5%–2% of paying customers file on Reclame Aqui (typical for trial-to-subscription models, a non-validated assumption), ~110 complaints/month at MCP implies about 5,5k–22k trial purchases per month, or roughly 180–730 per day, for that brand alone. Adding Zety BR, LiveCareer BR, Online Currículo and others, the BR paid market plausibly reaches hundreds to a few thousand transactions per day. **This is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a verified figure.**
- The complaint/sales ratio is also a warning: a business model that depends on surprise renewals creates RA reputation risk and chargebacks.

### Gaps
- No public counts for CVwizard, Currículo Fácil, resume.io, Kickresume, or Novorésumé on RA. Reclame Aqui was unreachable for direct checking.

## 6. Benchmarks globais

### Takeaway
The US market is large (about US$750M/year according to a competitor's lawsuit) and concentrated in BOLD (an alleged 80–90% share). The standard model is a US$1,95–2,95 trial that renews at US$24–30 every 4 weeks. Trial-to-paid conversion can be high (34% at StandOut CV), but visitor-to-paid is low (around 2% in small cases).

### Cited Findings
- **Rocket Resume v. BOLD Limited** (N.D. Cal., filed Apr/2026, antitrust): alleges BOLD holds 80–90% of the US online résumé-builder market, which it puts at roughly US$750M+/year. These are one party's allegations in a pending suit. — [Rocket Resume](https://rocket-resume.com/rocket-resume-v-bold-antitrust-lawsuit); [Haired.app review](https://www.haired.app/blog/zety-review-2026)
- **BOLD revenue:** conflicting estimates of US$628M (ZoomInfo) and US$272M (Growjo). BOLD is private and publishes no figures. [TERCEIROS] — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/bold-ltd/545888996); [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/BOLD)
- BOLD won the US$28,4M bid for the CareerBuilder and Monster assets (Jul/Aug 2025). — [JobBoardSecrets](https://www.jobboardsecrets.com/2025/07/21/bolds-ascendancy-in-the-online-recruitment-ecosystem/); [Wikipedia CareerBuilder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CareerBuilder)
- Zety US pricing (2026): US$1,95 trial, renewing at US$25,95 every 4 weeks, or US$71,40/year. The free plan exports only .txt. [TERCEIROS] — [ResuFit](https://resufit.com/pt/blog/zety-avaliacao-precos-vale-a-pena/index.md)
- Resume.io: US$2,95 for 7 days, then about US$29,95/month. Rezi: US$29/month or US$149 lifetime. Teal+: US$13/week. Kickresume: about US$8/month on annual billing. [TERCEIROS] — [Kickresume comparativo](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/best-ai-resume-builders/); [DigitalTatva](https://digitaltatva.com/best-ai-resume-builders/)

- **Dutch CV builder SaaS:** €3.784 MRR, 210 subscribers, 75% churn, 18% account-to-paid. — [LinkedIn post](https://nl.linkedin.com/in/kevinvdburgt)
- **JobSprout (2026):** 1.350 users, US$200 MRR (about 2%). — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/jobsprout)

### Inferences
- 75% churn and high trial-to-paid rates indicate a "one-time purchase disguised as a subscription" market: people want the PDF once. A R$9,90–29,90 one-off model fits real behavior better and carries less legal and reputational risk, but gives up the renewal revenue that funds the CAC of the big players.

### Gaps
- Verified revenue for Kickresume, Teal, Rezi and resume.io. FTC actions (the FTC "click-to-cancel" rule and its status in 2025/26 were not researched here).

## 7. Contexto do mercado de trabalho BR 2026 / risco regulatório

### Takeaway
Unemployment is at a historic low (5,4%–6,1% in 2026, about 5,9–6,2M unemployed), which shrinks the pool of desperate job seekers but does not remove job switching. Under the CDC, automatic renewal without express consent is treated as abusive (Idec, Procon-PR).

### Cited Findings
- PNAD: quarter ending Jan/2026 at 5,4%, about 5,9M unemployed (series low). Quarter ending Feb/2026 at 5,8%, 6,2M. Quarter ending Mar/2026 at 6,1%. Quarter ending Jun/2026 at 5,4% (series low for a June quarter, per ABIMAQ citing IBGE). — [IBGE fev](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-sala-de-imprensa/46206-pnad-continua-taxa-de-desocupacao-e-de-5-8-e-taxa-de-subutilizacao-e-de-14-1-no-trimestre-encerrado-em-fevereiro.html); [IBGE mar](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/46515-no-trimestre-encerrado-em-marco-desocupacao-sobe-para-6-1.html); [Band jan](https://www.band.com.br/economia/noticias/desemprego-fica-em-54-no-trimestre-encerrado-em-janeiro-de-2026-202603051003); [ABIMAQ](https://abimaq.org.br//wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026080632044001786529336.pdf)
- Gupy (the dominant BR ATS): cleanup rounds removed 43.046 abandoned job postings and resolved 6.168.587 applications cumulatively (LinkedIn post, Aug/2026, unofficial). — [LinkedIn](https://br.linkedin.com/in/anunes1)
- CDC art. 39, III, and paragraph 3: Idec says renewal requires express and positive consent, an automatic-renewal clause is void, and art. 42 provides a double refund of improper charges. Procon-PR cites a résumé registration site as an example of a free trial that turns into a card charge. — [Idec](https://www.idec.org.br/em-acao/em-foco/renovacao-automatica-de-assinaturas-e-considerada-pratica-abusiva); [Jurídico Certo](https://juridicocerto.com/p/paula-goulart-ferrei/artigos/a-renovacao-automatica-de-assinaturas-a-pratica-proibida-e-o-direito-do-consumidor-6287)

### Gaps
- CAGED 2026 (total hires and separations, roughly 2M+ hires per month historically) not collected here. No official data on the share of applications going through an ATS or on total Gupy applications per year.

## 8. Veredito

### Takeaway
**Yes, there is solid evidence that Brazilians pay** for résumé builders: BOLD runs at least 3–4 pt-BR brands with BRL billing, and Meu Currículo Perfeito alone generates ~110 complaints per month. But much of that "payment" is R$5–9 trials followed by renewals the customer did not want. 100–150 sales/day for a newcomer is **plausible but not proven**, and it depends on outbidding BOLD in paid search.

### Cited Findings
- Price and complaint evidence: see sections 1–2 (Reclame Aqui, Portal da Queixa, BOLD's App Store listing).

### Inferences
- [INFERÊNCIA] Demand math: 170k searches/month, roughly 5,7k/day. Even capturing 30% of that traffic (organic plus ads) at 3–5% payment conversion gives about 50–85 sales/day. **Reaching 100–150/day would require dominant SEO plus heavy paid acquisition, or broader terms ("currículo pdf", "currículo jovem aprendiz", "currículo ATS Gupy").**
- [INFERÊNCIA] Unit economics: at R$0,35–4 CPC and 3% conversion, CAC is about R$12–130 per sale. A one-off R$9,90 ticket does not cover the expensive CPCs. R$19,90–29,90, or upsells (cover letter, ATS review, LinkedIn), become necessary. BOLD covers CAC with recurring renewals at R$33–50.
- **Main risks:** (1) free alternatives (Canva, Word, Google Docs, free apps, Catho/Gupy built-in résumés) and an audience with low purchasing power; (2) BOLD concentration and bidding power in search; (3) CDC/Procon/Reclame Aqui exposure if copying the trial-to-auto-renew model, which is the very model generating the complaints; (4) unemployment at a historic low (~5,4%); (5) LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini) writing résumés for free; (6) high churn and strong seasonality in the January–March peak.
- **Opportunity:** honest positioning ("pague uma vez, sem assinatura, PDF ATS-Gupy") directly addresses the market's main pain, as shown in the complaints.

### Gaps
- Validate with a quick test: a landing page plus R$300–500 of Google Ads on "fazer currículo"/"currículo online", measuring CPC, CTR and checkout conversion before building the full product.
