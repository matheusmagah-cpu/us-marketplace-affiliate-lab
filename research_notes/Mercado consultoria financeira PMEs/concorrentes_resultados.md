# Concorrentes com resultados reais: consultoria/mentoria/BPO financeiro para clínicas e PMEs no Brasil (2023–2026)

> Method note: ~20 web searches were run on 2026-10-25. Direct page fetches (WebFetch) failed with DNS errors for most target sites (sucessodonto.com.br, sucessodontoselect.com.br, clinicorp.com, bra1.com.br, bposuite.com.br, gestaoapollo.com.br). Most findings below therefore come from search-result snippets and summaries of those pages. Treat exact wording and numbers as "as reported in search snippet". Almost every number is **self-declared marketing** unless marked otherwise. I found no audited or independent revenue data for any player.

## Q1. Who are the notable players (clinics and SMEs)?

### Takeaway
In dental, one player is far ahead of the rest in visible scale: **SucessOdonto / Grupo ICOM (Ricardo Novack)**. It combines a big yearly event, a 12-month high-ticket mentoring program (Select), a perpetual masterclass funnel and a partnership with the software vendor Clinicorp. Aesthetic and medical clinics are much more fragmented: many small Hotmart mentors and boutique consultancies. For SMEs, BPO financeiro is a crowded, commoditized field (1,300+ providers on oHub, 1,183 Conta Azul partners) with franchise networks (Marvee, iFinance), while CFO-as-a-service is a smaller premium niche.

### Inferences
- In dental, only SucessOdonto shows independent-looking traction signals: a 10-year recurring event with thousands of attendees, a software-vendor partnership and multiple press pieces. Everyone else is a micro-player. In aesthetics and medicine there is no dominant "financial-management" brand. The space is open.
- Clinic mentors sell **management plus marketing plus sales**, not pure finance. Pure-finance offers in health are mostly small BPOs or low-ticket courses. A "finance-first" positioning for clinics is differentiated but must be connected to revenue and profit outcomes.

### Gaps
- No independent ranking, revenue data or client counts for any clinic mentor. All are self-declared.
- Could not fetch pages directly (DNS failures), so team size, Instagram follower counts and detailed testimonials were not verified.
- Did not identify notable named "aceleradoras de clínicas" with a formal finance program. Search returned none.
- Big generalist SME education players (e.g., G4 Educação) were not researched in this pass.

## Q2. What they sell: formats, deliverables, duration, prices

### Takeaway
Clinic mentors follow a classic Brazilian infoproduct ladder: a free or cheap entry point (masterclass, "sessão diagnóstico" call, Hotmart course at roughly R$40–R$1k), then an event or immersion (~R$1k per day), then a 12-month high-ticket group mentoring program with a guarantee. BPO is sold as a monthly retainer (~R$600–R$2.5k for small firms). CFO-as-a-service runs about R$5k–R$30k/month. Sebrae anchors the low end (R$400–R$1.1k for 8–15h consulting).

### Cited Findings
- **SucessOdonto Select:** 12-month mentoring with a dentist mentor who has "minimum R$6M/year revenue". Claims to have helped 2,500+ clinics bill above R$100k. Offers a refund guarantee if the student doesn't bill a multiple of the amount paid. The price is not public. Entry is via a free "sessão diagnóstico/consultoria gratuita VIP" with Ricardo Novack — [Select](https://sucessodontoselect.com.br/); [Sessão diagnóstico](https://sucessodontoselect.com.br/consultoria-gratuita-vip-ricardo-novack/)
- SucessOdonto funnel pieces: a perpetual masterclass "Clínicas Que Fazem Milhões", a "SucessOdonto 300k" program page, an "Imersão 2026" launch page and an in-person sales page — [Masterclass](https://sucessodonto.com.br/masterclass-perpetuo-1/); [300k](https://sucessodonto.com.br/sucesso-odonto-300-k/); [Imersão 2026](https://lp.sucessodontoselect.com.br/venda-lan); [venda presencial](https://sucessodontoselect.com.br/venda-presencial/)
- Mentoria Odonto Express: anchor "from R$4,997 for R$0" on one part of the page and "from R$997 for 12x" elsewhere (inconsistent). Includes a cash-flow and indicators tool as a bonus — [Odonto Express](https://mentoriaodontoexpress.com.br/)
- Gestão de Estética immersion: R$1,097 for a one-day ticket — [gestaodeestetica.com](https://www.gestaodeestetica.com/imersao-em-gestao-e-marketing/)
- Sublime aesthetics finance course: R$39.62 shown on the page (unclear whether this is an installment) — [Sublime](https://sublimemed.com.br/sublime-gestao-financeira-para-estetica/)

- Aesthetic Hotmart mentors frame offers around revenue targets (R$10k, R$30k, R$50k/month) and small groups (~10 people, weekly Google Meet, 2h/week) — Hotmart links in Q1.
- **BPO financeiro prices (SMEs, provider blogs, 2025–2026):** entry ~R$600/month; from R$750/month for microenterprises; R$1,200–1,800/month for 50–200 entries/month (another source: R$1,800–4,500 for the same volume); a Campinas firm quotes R$900–1,800/month; a Santander blog cites R$800–2,500 for micro and small firms. ### Inferences
- Price ladder to copy: free diagnostic call → low-ticket course or one-day immersion (~R$1k) → 12-month program (high ticket, price hidden, sold on a call) → optional done-for-you BPO retainer.
- Sebrae's subsidized R$400–1,100 consulting sets a low price anchor for "diagnóstico financeiro". A paid diagnostic must deliver clearly more, such as implementation, a dashboard or a clinic-specific benchmark.
- Health clinics need specialized finance (insurer claim denials, physician payout splits, cost per chair-hour, financed treatment plans), which justifies a premium over generic BPO.

### Gaps
- No public price for SucessOdonto Select, MBH, Clínicas de Elite or most high-ticket programs. Typical Brazilian high-ticket mentoring prices (often R$15k–R$60k+) could not be confirmed from sources here.
- No independent survey of BPO prices (e.g., from accounting associations). All ranges come from vendors.
- Brasil Mais program details (financial-management module, current status) were not found.

## Q3. Signals of traction, acquisition channels, and gaps or weaknesses to exploit

### Takeaway
The proven acquisition engine in dental is **event + perpetual webinar/masterclass + free diagnostic call + software-vendor partnership + PR**, all used by SucessOdonto. BPO firms acquire through marketplaces (oHub, Conta Azul partner directory), accountant channels and SEO content. Weak points across the market: unverifiable revenue promises, marketing/sales-heavy curricula with thin finance depth, no hard financial KPIs reported, and regulatory risk for ads that promise results in healthcare.

### Cited Findings
- **Events:** SucessOdonto's yearly in-person event is in its 10th edition, expecting ~6,200 attendees in 2026 and 30,000+ cumulative. The 2023 edition was promoted as "the largest management and marketing dental event" — [Brasil Inovador](https://brasilinovador.com.br/gestao-corporativa-na-saude-impulsiona-mercado-odontologico-e-projeta-encontro-bilionario-em-campinas/); [SucessOdonto Presencial 2023](https://sucessodontopresencial.com.br/evento/icomtv/)
- **Software-vendor partnership:** Clinicorp (32,000+ clinics in its dataset) formally partners with SucessOdonto and publishes co-marketing content. Clinicorp also partners with other consultants (e.g., "Encantadores de Pacientes") — [Clinicorp & SucessOdonto](https://clinicorp.com/post/sucessodonto-clinicorp); [Clinicorp parceiros](https://www.clinicorp.com/post/parceiros-produtividade-financeira-odontologia-encantadores-de-pacientes); [Tribuna Carioca](https://tribunacarioca.com.br/2026/09/faturamento-com-estetica-em-clinicas-odontologicas-cresce-366/)
- **PR placements:** several 2025–2026 articles repeat ICOM's revenue figures (BRA 1, Brasil Inovador, EGO Brazil). This is an "earned/paid media" credibility tactic — links in Q1.
- **Funnel assets:** perpetual masterclass, free VIP diagnostic session, launch-style immersion pages, in-person sales — SucessOdonto links in Q2.
- **Software as a channel in aesthetics:** the Gestão de Estética speaker is a partner in software for 1,000+ clinics — [gestaodeestetica.com](https://www.gestaodeestetica.com/imersao-em-gestao-e-marketing/)
- **Marketplaces and directories for BPO:** oHub (1,300+ BPO providers, up to 4 quotes per request); Conta Azul partner directory (1,183 partners). Conta Azul also publishes "how to offer and how much to charge for BPO" content aimed at accountants — [oHub](https://www.ohub.com.br/empresas/bpo-financeiro/28); [Conta Azul](https://contaazul.com/encontre-contador/bpo-financeiro/); [Conta Azul parceiros blog](https://contaazul.com/blog/parceiros/bpo-financeiro-como-comecar-qual-importancia-para-sua-empresa/)
- **Market tailwind data usable in content:** Clinicorp data shows aesthetic-procedure revenue in dental and aesthetic clinics grew 36.6% (Jan–Aug 2026 vs 2025) — [Tribuna Carioca](https://tribunacarioca.com.br/2026/09/faturamento-com-estetica-em-clinicas-odontologicas-cresce-366/). Franchising overall: 202,444 units and 3,297 networks in 2025 — [Seu Dinheiro](https://www.seudinheiro.com/2026/seu-negocio/franquias-brasileiras-crescem-105-em-2025-e-ultrapassam-r-300-bilhoes-pela-primeira-vez-cbcb/)
- **Reputation:** no Reclame Aqui profile was found for SucessOdonto in search. Searches for BPO complaints on Reclame Aqui returned nothing usable — [search result context: Reclame Aqui OdontoCompany page unrelated](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/odonto-company/)
- **Regulatory angle (dental ads):** CFO Resolution 196/2019 prohibits guarantees of results, superlatives and patient testimonials in dental advertising. Sources conflict on price advertising after a March 2025 CADE decision (Clinicorp says CFO can no longer punish promotions; HBA Marketing 2026 says art. 32 still prohibits prices). This applies to the clinics' own ads, which mentors teach — [Sorriai](https://sorriai.com.br/blog/resolucao-cfo-196-guia); [Clinicorp CADE](https://www.clinicorp.com/post/promocao-odontologia); [HBA](https://hbamarketing.com.br/o-que-dentista-pode-anunciar-regras-cfo/)
- **Weaknesses visible in offers:** Hotmart aesthetic mentors' revenue promises (R$10k in 90 days, R$30k/month) carry no supporting evidence on the pages — [Hotmart search summary, e.g.](https://hotmart.com/pt-br/marketplace/produtos/mentoria-empodere-seu-negocio-de-estetica/I50108707U). Odonto Express shows inconsistent anchor prices on one page — [Odonto Express](https://mentoriaodontoexpress.com.br/). SucessOdonto's reach numbers are internally inconsistent (7,500 vs 15,000) — Q1 links. The Acelerah/SALTO "accelerator" is sales-focused, not finance — [SALTO](https://eventos.congresse.me/salto). BPO case studies are anonymous ("an online store improved cash flow 40%") and unverifiable — [Equipe BPO](https://equipebpo.com.br/bpo-financeiro-pmes-guia-completo-2026/)
- Pain points providers emphasize: dentists mixing personal and clinic accounts; clinics that "bill well but don't profit"; medical-clinic claim denials and physician payout splits — [Prone](https://prone.com.br/bpo-financeiro-para-odontologia/); [Alessandro Freitas](https://alessandrofreitas.com/); [iBPO](https://www.ibpo.com.br/terceirizacao-financeira/clinicas-medicas)

### Inferences
- What works (copy): (1) a large annual event as the trust engine; (2) a free "diagnóstico" call as the entry to a high-ticket sale; (3) partnerships with clinic software (Clinicorp, aesthetics software) for distribution and data-driven content; (4) PR with aggregate "student revenue" numbers; (5) a guarantee tied to a multiple of the price.
- How to position against them: lead with **profit, not revenue** (margin per procedure, cost per chair-hour, pro-labore, tax regime optimization). Report verifiable before/after KPIs with client consent. Bundle mentoring with done-for-you BPO (no visible clinic mentor does this at scale; health BPOs don't mentor). Offer a finance-specialist alternative to the marketing-heavy mentors.
- Risk: ads that promise revenue to health professionals invite skepticism. Clinic clients also operate under advertising restrictions, so teaching compliant marketing is a value-add.
- For SMEs in general, BPO is commoditized at ~R$1–2.5k/month. Differentiation comes from niche specialization (e.g., clinics) or moving up to fractional CFO (R$5–15k).

### Gaps
- Meta Ad Library could not be accessed (no fetch). Specific ad angles, creatives and spend for SucessOdonto and others are unknown.
- Instagram/YouTube follower counts and engagement were not retrieved.
- Presence at CIOSP and partnerships with dental associations, accountants or suppliers were not verified (apart from Clinicorp).
- Complaints (Reclame Aqui, Procon, lawsuits) about clinic mentors and BPOs were not found. This should not be read as evidence that none exist.
