# Funis e ofertas para consultoria B2B high-ticket (R$5k–15k) vendida a donos de PMEs com tráfego pago frio — Brasil

Research date: 2026-10-25. About 14 search/fetch calls. Note: owlclaw.com and jamesschramko.com could not be fetched (DNS failure), so claims from those pages come from search-result snippets only. Most "benchmarks" in this space come from vendors or agencies and use differing definitions. Each item below is labeled **[DATA]** (measured dataset with a stated sample), **[VENDOR]** (vendor or agency claim, method unclear) or **[OPINION]** (practitioner framework or anecdote).

## 1. Funnel comparison: paid diagnostic vs application + call vs webinar/VSL vs lead magnet + nurture vs outbound/partnerships (cold traffic, no social proof)

### Takeaway
No independent study compares these five models head to head for solo consultants. The evidence is indirect: what is known about form-to-meeting conversion, no-show rates, quiz opt-in rates and webinar attendance. That evidence, plus practitioner frameworks, favors short, low-friction funnels with instant booking or a low-priced paid diagnostic over long webinar or nurture sequences when the budget is small and there is no social proof. Outbound and partnerships are the cheapest way to get the first conversations, but there are almost no hard numbers for them in Brazil.

### Cited Findings
- **Paid diagnostic [OPINION]:** James Schramko's "paid diagnostic" playbook has three steps. First a qualification form, then a short free call whose only purpose is to sell the diagnostic, then a paid 60–90-minute session priced at US$500–800. The fee is credited toward the full program if the prospect becomes a client. He argues that prospects who refuse to answer qualifying questions were unlikely buyers anyway. No conversion rate is published. — [James Schramko, Paid Diagnostic playbook](https://jamesschramko.com/playbooks/paid-diagnostic/) (from search snippet; the page could not be fetched)
- **Productized diagnostic [OPINION]:** Wayfront argues that a defined-scope diagnostic packages well because it has a "clear start, clear end, clear deliverable." It suggests charging for the discovery work consultants already do for free, and says open-ended advisory work packages poorly. — [Wayfront, Productized consulting](https://wayfront.com/blog/productized-consulting)
- **Market examples of paid diagnostics [VENDOR]:** Upwork sellers offer a 60-minute diagnostic plus action plan for about US$500. Microsoft Marketplace lists 1–2-week paid "diagnostic and roadmap" engagements. — [Upwork example](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/marketing-revenue-growth-diagnostic-conversion-aov-funnel-performance-2039021678872378788); [Microsoft Marketplace](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/pt-pt/product/sunrisetechnologiesinc1697390378931.d365-businesscentral-diagnostics)
- **Instant booking on the form (application + call) [VENDOR/DATA]:** Across about 4 million form submissions, Chili Piper reports that form-to-meeting conversion rises from about 30% to about 66.7% when the scheduler appears right at form submission. — reported via [Komo glossary](https://komo.ai/glossary/calendar-booking) / [Cometly](https://www.cometly.com/academy/no-show-rate-by-source)
- **Webinar attendance [DATA]:** ON24's 2025 report (2024 data) puts registration-to-attendance at 57% for B2B webinars, and the 2026 report at 60%. ON24 also says on-demand viewing accounts for about 50% of attendees. — [ON24 Benchmarks takeaways](https://www.on24.com/blog/key-takeaways-from-the-webinar-benchmarks-report/). Goldcast measures only 33% attendance among registrants, using a different definition (single-session webinars of 90 minutes or less). — [Goldcast 2025 stats](https://www.goldcast.io/blog-post/2025-webinar-statistics-b2b-marketers-should-know). Neither source gives a webinar-to-sales-call conversion rate. Note that both datasets are enterprise B2B (mostly warm audiences), not cold traffic to small businesses.
- **Quiz / interactive diagnostic as a lead magnet [DATA]:** Interact reports that 40.1% of people who start a quiz become leads and 65% of starters finish it. The sample is 3,568 quizzes with at least 10 leads each, from the 6 months ending 17 Nov 2023 (older data). Interact says this rate has been stable since 2013. — [Interact Quiz Conversion Rate Report](https://www.tryinteract.com/blog/quiz-conversion-rate-report/). ConvertFlow (Sept 2026, 1,063 quiz funnel campaigns) measures against all visitors instead: quiz landing pages turn 11.28% of visitors into leads, and lead-gen quiz funnels turn 7.85% of visitors into leads while engaging 23.84%. — [ConvertFlow quiz funnel benchmark Sept 2026](https://www.convertflow.com/benchmark-reports/lead-generation-quiz-funnel-benchmark-september-2026)
- **Outbound in Brazil [VENDOR]:** A Snov.io study (May 2025–May 2026, about 53.9 million emails in BR and US) finds that multichannel outreach (email + LinkedIn + phone/WhatsApp) gets 85% more replies than email alone in Brazil, while email alone does better in the US. — [Snov.io, Web Summit Rio](https://snov.io/blog/br/pesquisa-web-summit-rio-outreach-multicanal/)
- **WhatsApp [VENDOR]:** An Ollow study of more than 84,000 businesses claims an 81% response rate on WhatsApp. The methodology could not be verified. — cited in [Snov.io/ABC da Comunicação coverage](https://www.abcdacomunicacao.com.br/?p=351082)
- **LinkedIn prospecting rules of thumb [OPINION]:** One Brazilian guide cites 20–35% connection acceptance and 5–10% first-message replies when messages are genuinely personalized, with no primary source. It also warns that sending 200 generic WhatsApp messages without segmentation is a common mistake, and that relying only on referrals is risky. — [Inteligência Setorial, prospecção ativa para prestadores de serviço](https://inteligenciasetorial.com.br/estrategias-de-prospeccao-ativa-para-prestadores-de-servicos/)
- **Inbound contact behavior in Brazil [DATA, survey]:** RD Station's Panorama 2025 (more than 3,800 respondents, 90% confidence level, 2% margin of error) finds that most leads arrive through website or blog forms. For the Health & Aesthetics segment, the WhatsApp button is more relevant. — [RD Station, Canais de Marketing e Operação](https://www.rdstation.com/pesquisas/panorama-marketing-vendas/edicao-2026/marketing/canais-operacao/); [Panoramas RD Station 2025](https://www.rdstation.com/pesquisas/panoramas-rdstation-2025/)
- **WhatsApp redirect [VENDOR survey]:** A 2025 survey cited by Agendor says 78.78% of companies redirect leads to WhatsApp at some stage of the conversion journey. — [Agendor, panorama de geração de leads](https://www.agendor.com.br/blog/panorama-de-geracao-de-leads-no-brasil/)

### Inferences
- For someone with **no cases, a small budget and cold traffic**, the evidence points to this hybrid: **Meta Ads → short interactive diagnostic (quiz or calculator) → result page with instant booking (scheduler embedded) → handoff on WhatsApp → free 30–45-min call that sells a paid diagnostic (R$300–1,000, credited toward the project) → project R$5k–15k.**
  - The quiz/calculator raises opt-in rates (higher than static PDF lead magnets per Interact/ConvertFlow) and gives the consultant qualification data.
  - Instant booking roughly doubles lead→meeting (Chili Piper).
  - The paid diagnostic is a smaller "yes" that builds proof (deliverables and early testimonials) and lowers perceived risk while the consultant has no social proof. This logic is practitioner opinion, not measured data.
- A webinar or VSL needs volume: about 40–60% of registrants never attend, and each attendee is expensive. With R$1.5–5k/month, a solo consultant will see only a few dozen registrants per month, which is too few to learn from. Treat it as low priority for this phase.
- Lead magnet + long nurture works, but the payoff takes weeks or months. That fits poorly with "first sales soon" unless it ends in a direct booking CTA.
- Outbound (WhatsApp/LinkedIn/phone) and partnerships with accountants cost little cash and need no ad-algorithm learning. They are probably the fastest route to the first 3–5 clients and should run **alongside** paid traffic, not as a replacement. There are no Brazil-specific conversion numbers for this, so the founder has to measure it.

### Gaps
- No public dataset gives the conversion rate from paid diagnostic to full project, in Brazil or elsewhere.
- No head-to-head study of funnel models for solo consultants selling to small businesses.
- No data on referral partnerships with accountants (conversion, typical commission).
- No data on webinar/VSL → call → close for cold traffic aimed at small-business owners.

## 2. Benchmarks: CPL on Meta Ads (Brazil, B2B / business owners), show rate, close rate, cost per booked call, CAC

### Takeaway
No reliable Brazil-specific B2B CPL benchmark exists in public sources. Approximate ranges, mostly from vendors:
- Meta CPL for B2B or business-owner offers in Brazil: low tens of reais up to around R$100+ (inferred, see below)
- No-show on booked calls: 6.5% (warm, instant booking) to 20–35% (typical)
- High-ticket close rate on qualified calls: 10–30%

Treat all of these as hypotheses to check against the consultant's own numbers.

### Cited Findings
- **Meta CPL, Brazil, all sectors [VENDOR aggregate]:** Superads reports Brazil's 2025 average CPL as well below the global average: annual average "21.0", ranging from 64.22 (May) to 3.30 (Nov). The currency and unit are ambiguous (the page appears to report in USD), so treat this as very rough. — [Superads, CPL Brazil](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-lead/brazil)
- **Meta CPL, Brazil, software development (closest B2B proxy) [VENDOR aggregate]:** Average 52.51 (about 40% above global), monthly median 14.93 (vs 38.59 globally). The average is skewed by one outlier month. — [Superads, software development Brazil](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-lead/software-development/brazil)
- **Meta ad tax increase in Brazil [news]:** From 2026, Meta passes 12.15% in taxes on to Brazilian advertisers, which pushes CPL up. — [Central do Varejo](https://centraldovarejo.com.br/aumento-nos-anuncios/)
- **Meta CPL by industry (US, LocaliQ Oct 2025) [DATA, US]:** Ranges from about US$3 for restaurants to about US$77 for dentists (US$76.71 CPL, US$9.78 CPC). — [Influee summary of LocaliQ](https://influee.co/br/blog/how-much-do-facebook-ads-cost); [Webtonic orthodontics](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/orthodontics-meta-ads-statistics)
- **Meta vs LinkedIn CPL (B2B) [VENDOR]:** A 2026 comparison table puts LinkedIn B2B CPL at US$50–200 and Meta at about US$30–80. Metadata.io (2025): LinkedIn native lead forms cost US$193/lead (97 advertisers) vs US$346/lead for landing-page campaigns (65 advertisers). — [Expandi LinkedIn Ads](https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-ads/); [Metadata.io benchmark](https://metadata.io/benchmark-report-2026/insights/forms-beat-pages)
- **Show / no-show rates:**
  - RevenueHero (Dec 2024, 6,428 B2B meetings) [DATA]: overall no-show 6.5%. The sample is skewed toward warm leads using purpose-built schedulers.
  - Chili Piper [VENDOR]: typical sales-meeting no-show is 20–35%.
  - Calendly [VENDOR]: automated reminders cut no-shows by about 28%, and 88% of its sales users saw no-shows fall.
  - Focus Digital [VENDOR, methodology unclear]: blended B2B no-show about 25%, versus about 8% with instant scheduling at form submission.
  - The widely repeated "20% standard no-show" figure has no primary research behind it.
  - Sources for this item: [Cometly, no-show rate by source](https://www.cometly.com/academy/no-show-rate-by-source); [Naoma, demo no-shows](https://naoma.ai/blog/article-01-demo-no-shows); [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-no-show-rate-for-booked-sales-meetings-by-booking-channel/); [Funnelfeedr, show rate](https://funnelfeedr.com/blog/show-rate)
- **Close rate, high ticket [VENDOR/OPINION]:** OwlClaw benchmark tables (2025) give a 10–25% close rate from qualified leads and a 30–50% "phone/video call conversion" (a different metric). The method is not documented. — [OwlClaw high-ticket lead benchmarks](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/high-ticket-lead-benchmarks) (snippet only). Job postings for high-ticket closers ask for a close rate of 30% or more on held calls and a show rate of 70% or more. These are employer targets, not averages. — [Remotive closer job](https://remotive.com/remote/jobs/sales/high-ticket-closer-5403087); [Wuzzuf setter job](https://wuzzuf.net/ar/jobs/p/dmoeknr8c8d8-high-ticket-appointment-setter-rje-consulting-llc-cairo-egypt)
- **Google Ads CPL (US, LocaliQ) [DATA, US]:**
  - 2025 report (16,446 campaigns): average CPL US$70.11 and CPC US$5.26; business services CPL US$103.54 (among the highest).
  - 2026 report (13,474 campaigns, Apr 2025–Mar 2026): average CPC US$5.42, CPL US$66.69.
  - Sources: [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ads-costs-rise-again-but-conversions-improve-report-455663); [WordStream benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/resources/google-ads-industry-benchmarks); [AdWave](https://adwave.com/resources/google-ads-cost-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- **Worked funnel model (illustrative, built from the ranges above, not measured):**
  - Assume a Meta CPL of R$30–80 for a well-segmented quiz aimed at clinic owners. This is inferred from the Superads median and the 12.15% tax increase; there is no direct source.
  - Assume 30–60% of leads book a call (instant booking) and 65–80% of booked calls are held.
  - That gives a cost per held call of roughly R$60–400.
  - With a 10–25% close rate (first-time seller with no cases: assume the low end, about 10%), CAC comes to about R$600–4,000 per client.
- With an R$1.5–5k/month budget, that suggests roughly 1–4 clients per month in the optimistic case and possibly zero in the first month while the consultant learns. The R$5–15k ticket supports this CAC, but the sample size is small.
- Make "cost per held call" and "cost per sale" the primary metrics, not CPL. Cheap Meta leads tend to be lower intent (see section 3).

### Gaps
- No Brazilian benchmark for Meta CPL among business owners, financial consulting or clinic owners (B2B). The Superads currency unit is unclear.
- No independent, verified source for close rate on high-ticket consulting calls.
- No public data on cost per booked call or CAC for consulting sold to Brazilian small businesses.

## 3. Channels: Meta vs Google vs Instagram organic vs LinkedIn vs Taboola/native for reaching small-business and clinic owners in Brazil

### Takeaway
Meta is the cheapest per lead and has the best reach among Brazilian clinic owners, who are heavy WhatsApp and Instagram users, but its intent is lower. Google brings higher intent but few people search for "consultoria financeira para clínica," and CPCs for service keywords are high. LinkedIn costs several times more per lead and reaches fewer clinic owners in Brazil. No evidence was found supporting Taboola/native for B2B high-ticket in Brazil.

### Cited Findings
- **Brazilian CPC reference (Stone, 2025) [VENDOR, no methodology]:** Google Ads R$1.50–5.00 per click and Meta Ads R$0.50–2.00. "Clínica odontológica perto de mim" has an average CPC of R$6–12. Those keywords target patients, but they indicate auction pressure in the clinic niche. — [Stone, quanto custa tráfego pago](https://conteudo.stone.com.br/quanto-custa-investir-em-trafego-pago/)
- **Google vs Meta in orthodontics (US) [DATA, US]:** Google CPL US$72.97 (10.67% conversion rate) vs Meta US$76.71. — [Webtonic orthodontics](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/orthodontics-meta-ads-statistics)
- **LinkedIn vs Meta [VENDOR]:**
  - LinkedIn CPL is US$50–200, versus about US$30–80 on Meta, and LinkedIn CPCs are several times higher. Meta leads tend to come from "lower-friction" offers. — [Expandi](https://expandi.io/blog/linkedin-ads/); [Nav43 LinkedIn benchmarks](https://nav43.com/blog/2025-linkedin-ads-benchmarks-every-saas-tech-marketer-needs)
  - Dreamdata 2025: LinkedIn €154 per company influenced vs Meta €299. Meta accounted for only 4% of MQLs, 3% of SQLs and 2% of new business in that B2B dataset (mostly SaaS and enterprise). — [PPC Land on Dreamdata](https://ppc.land/linkedin-emerges-as-top-b2b-platform-with-113-roas-and-211-day-buyer-journeys/)
- **Health & Aesthetics segment in Brazil [DATA, survey]:** The WhatsApp button matters more as a conversion channel in this segment. — [RD Station Panorama](https://www.rdstation.com/pesquisas/panorama-marketing-vendas/edicao-2026/marketing/canais-operacao/)
- **Lead quality vs cost (dental case, 2025) [anecdote]:** In a Meta campaign, high-ticket services had a CPL of US$12.71 versus US$3.53 for popular ones, but the cheaper leads booked appointments at a lower rate. A cheap CPL does not guarantee a cheap appointment. — [Freelancehunt case](https://freelancehunt.com/en/showcase/work/meta-ads-lead-generation-for-dentistry/2024204.html)

### Inferences
- For clinic owners in Brazil: **Meta (Instagram feed/Reels/Stories) with a quiz or WhatsApp click-to-chat** should be the main channel. Add **Google Search** only on bottom-of-funnel terms like "consultoria financeira para clínicas" or "gestão financeira clínica odontológica" (low volume, high intent). Use **LinkedIn mostly for organic outreach**, not ads. Organic Instagram is the free place to build the authority the consultant lacks (cases, behind the scenes, diagnostics).
- Skip Taboola/native at this budget. It is built for scale and content-led offers, and no evidence supports it for this use case.

### Gaps
- No Brazil data on CPC or volume for keywords like "consultoria financeira para clínicas." Check Google Keyword Planner.
- No data on how many Brazilian clinic owners are on LinkedIn.
- No Taboola/Outbrain data for Brazilian B2B.

## 4. Interactive diagnostic tools and "financial leak calculator" lead magnets

### Takeaway
Interactive quizzes and calculators convert better than static lead magnets in vendor datasets: 40% of quiz starters become leads, or 8–11% of visitors. No published data was found specifically on "financial leak calculators." Their appeal (a personalized number showing money lost) is in line with the quiz logic, but that is inference.

### Cited Findings
- 40.1% of quiz starters become leads and 65% finish (Interact, n=3,568 quizzes, data to Nov 2023). — [Interact](https://www.tryinteract.com/blog/quiz-conversion-rate-report/)
- 7.85–11.28% of visitors become leads on quiz funnels (ConvertFlow, n=1,063 campaigns, Sept 2026). — [ConvertFlow](https://www.convertflow.com/benchmark-reports/lead-generation-quiz-funnel-benchmark-september-2026)
- A Dupple blog post cites an Interact figure of "2,100 quizzes" that does not match Interact's own page. Treat it as unreliable. — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/blog/quiz-funnels-vs-lead-magnets-which-one-wins-the-conversion-battle-in-2026)
- Outgrow (a calculator and quiz tool) lists 17 lead-generation quiz formats, including assessments and calculators. — [Outgrow](https://outgrow.co/blog/?p=66078)
- In RD Station's Brazil data, forms are the main lead-capture mechanism, which supports a form or quiz at the start of the funnel. — [RD Station](https://www.rdstation.com/pesquisas/panorama-marketing-vendas/edicao-2026/marketing/canais-operacao/)

### Inferences
- Tool options:
  - **Interact** and **Outgrow**, both paid in USD
  - **Typeform** (free tier with a response limit)
  - **Tally** (generous free tier, supports calculations/scoring)
  - **involve.me**
  - **Google Forms + Sheets** (free)
  - A custom HTML/JS calculator
  - Tool capabilities and prices were **not verified in this research**; check current plans.
- A "calculadora de vazamento financeiro" (e.g., "quanto sua clínica perde por mês com inadimplência / agenda ociosa / precificação errada") is a strong fit. It quantifies pain in R$, qualifies the lead (revenue, number of chairs) and leads naturally into "review this number with me in a diagnostic." This is not proven by data.

### Gaps
- No published conversion data for financial calculators in particular, or for the Brazilian market.

## 5. Guarantees, risk reversal and pilot pricing

### Takeaway
The evidence that guarantees increase sales is mostly anecdotal or from A/B tests in e-commerce and info-products (+21% to +29%), and there is a documented counterexample where a guarantee reduced sales. No rigorous study covers B2B services. For a newcomer with no social proof, the frameworks (Hormozi) recommend **conditional** guarantees. That is opinion.

### Cited Findings
- **[DATA, single A/B test]:** Conversion Rate Experts reported a 29% sales lift from a variant that mentioned a 60-day money-back guarantee. — [Conversion Rate Experts](https://conversion-rate-experts.com/button-microcopy-win-report/)
- **[OPINION/unverified]:**
  - Neil Patel is cited as finding a 21% lift from a guarantee, with no primary source. — [Spotio](https://spotio.com/?p=3612)
  - A direct-mail anecdote claims 20x more responses and sales-letter conversion rising from 10% to 40%. — [Michel Fortin, risk reversal](https://michelfortin.com/copywriting/risk-reversal/)
- **Counterexample [anecdote]:** KitchenAid prominently added a 90-day guarantee and saw a "double-digit" drop in sales. The explanation offered is that for already-trusted brands a guarantee can introduce doubt. — [Conversion.com podcast, The Guarantee Backfire](https://conversion.com/?p=8751)
- **[OPINION] Hormozi:** Guarantees are a pillar of the "Grand Slam Offer." Conditional guarantees ("if you do X, Y, Z and don't get A, B, C, we refund + give you ___") are better than unconditional ones. — [Growth Memo breakdown](https://newslettergrowthmemo.beehiiv.com/p/breaking-down-hormozi-s-100m-launch); [Max Mednik notes](https://www.maxmednik.com/blog/notes-on-100m-offers-by-alex-hormozi)
- **Diagnostic fee credited toward the project [OPINION]:** This is a form of partial risk reversal. — [Schramko](https://jamesschramko.com/playbooks/paid-diagnostic/)

### Inferences
- For a consultant with no cases, the risk-reversal options most consistent with these sources are:
  - (a) a **diagnostic fee credited** toward the project
  - (b) a **conditional guarantee** on the diagnostic, e.g., "se não identificarmos pelo menos R$X/mês de vazamento, devolvo 100%"
  - (c) **"founding client" pilot pricing** with a discount (e.g., 30–50%) in exchange for a testimonial or case study and permission to publish the numbers
  - The unknown brand is exactly the situation where a guarantee is more likely to help than hurt (the opposite of the KitchenAid case).
- Avoid unconditional guarantees on the full project when the consultant does not control the outcome (financial results depend on the client carrying out the work).

### Gaps
- Alan Weiss's view (options-based proposals, value-based fees) on guarantees was not found in this research.
- No study on service guarantees in B2B consulting or in Brazil.
- No data on the effect of "founding client" pilot discounts.

## 6. Free or cheap stack in Brazil (forms, scheduling, CRM, WhatsApp, payments, contracts)

### Takeaway
A free or almost-free stack is feasible. Prices were only partly verified: Pix is free for individuals but **may carry fees for companies depending on the bank**, and ZapSign has a free plan with a limit of about 5 documents per month (unofficial source). Check every price on the official sites.

### Cited Findings
- Pix: free to send and receive for individuals. For companies, the fee depends on the bank. — [Santander](https://www.santander.com.br/blog/pix-vai-ser-cobrado); [Meutudo](https://meutudo.com.br/blog/pix-taxado/)
- Asaas (billing: Pix, boleto, card) markets itself as having "the lowest fees" but no official fee table was found in the results. — [Blog Asaas](https://blog.asaas.com/pix-cobra-taxa)
- ZapSign (e-signature, works with WhatsApp):
  - Free plan with about 5 documents per month (third-party coupon site, not official). — [Descontos Top](https://descontostop.com/loja/cupom-desconto-zapsign/)
  - Paid plan around R$29.90/month (the official blog table is confusing). — [Blog ZapSign](https://blog.zapsign.com.br/en/quanto-custa-uma-assinatura-digital/)
  - The pricing page at zapsign.co shows USD pricing with a different structure. — [zapsign.co/pricing](https://zapsign.co/pricing)

### Inferences
- **Suggested stack (prices not verified in this research):**

  | Function | Suggested tools |
  |---|---|
  | Quiz / form | Tally or Typeform free / Google Forms |
  | Scheduling | Calendly free or Cal.com free, Google Calendar appointment schedules (with automatic reminders; Calendly reports about 28% fewer no-shows) |
  | CRM | Free pipeline in HubSpot, Notion or Trello; Brazilian options with trials: Agendor, RD Station CRM |
  | WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business app (free: labels, auto-replies, catalog) before paying for the API |
  | Payments | Asaas, Mercado Pago or Stripe/InfinitePay (Pix + card in installments; installments matter for a R$5–15k ticket) |
  | Contracts | ZapSign free / gov.br signature (free for individuals) |

### Gaps
- Official, current prices for Asaas, Calendly, Tally, Interact/Outgrow and the WhatsApp Business API in Brazil were not verified.
- No data comparing Brazilian CRMs for solo consultants.
