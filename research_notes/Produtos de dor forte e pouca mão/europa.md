Research date: 2026-10-25. Method: about 17 web searches and fetch attempts. Several fetches failed on DNS (support.google.com, relevantaudience.com, marketeer.sapo.pt), so some points rest on search-result summaries of those pages and are flagged.

**Labels used in these notes.** VERIFIED means a public source states it, cited inline. EST means my own estimate, not sourced: check it in Google Keyword Planner before spending. ## Q1. Which regulatory obligations create urgent, searchable pain for SMEs and online sellers, and which can be productized low-touch?

### Takeaway
The best fits are marketplace-seller compliance obligations: the GPSR responsible person and German packaging EPR (LUCID plus the new authorised-representative duty from 12 Aug 2026). In both, non-compliance gets listings blocked, prices are known (€150–2,000 a year), and the owner can resell or white-label licensed EU providers. Next come UK MTD for Income Tax (about 860k people mandated from Apr 2026) and Belgian Peppol e-invoicing (fines of €1,500, €3,000 and €5,000), but these work best as affiliate or setup plays. EAA accessibility has real demand, but deliverables are expensive and enforcement is still soft.

### Cited Findings

**GPSR: EU responsible person (Reg. (EU) 2023/988, applies since 13 Dec 2024)**
- Market price for a responsible person (RP) or authorised representative service is about €150–500 a year at the low end. Fivex quotes €500–2,000 a year depending on product families. Running your own EU entity costs €3,000–15,000 a year. — [euverify](https://euverify.com/resource/authorised-representative-costs-guide/); [Fivex](https://website.fivex.com/blog/gtm-gpsr-explained-chinese-sellers-manufacturers/)
- EAS charges tiered prices: €199 a year for 1 product type, €349 for 2, and €690 for up to 6. — [EAS GPSR](https://easproject.com/gpsr/); [EAS help](https://help.easproject.com/eas-gpsr-compliance-service)
- Webinterpret is listed from €400 a year, or a $30/month option. — [Webinterpret comparison](https://resources.webinterpret.com/compare-webinterpret-and-vatai/)
- On the Amazon Seller Forum, sellers report RP quotes of €650–2,000 a year as "very expensive", and one mentions about $200 a year. These are anecdotal, but they show sellers are actively shopping for this. — [Amazon Seller Central EU forum](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/d2c4b0b5-7e83-4d6d-9dd6-2d6254a65375)

**Packaging EPR in Germany (VerpackG, LUCID) and the PPWR authorised representative**
- The official German Packaging Register says that from 12 Aug 2026, a company abroad with no German branch must appoint an authorised representative. That representative must be entered in LUCID before the first sale. LUCID registration itself stays a personal duty the representative cannot take over. — [Verpackungsregister: authorised representative](https://www.verpackungsregister.org/en/ppwr/authorised-representative); [Verpackungsregister knowledge base](https://verpackungsregister.org/en/information-orientation/knowledge-base/authorising-a-representative)
- One commercial provider (ecosistant) charges €250 a year for up to 1 tonne of packaging for the authorised-representative service. — [ecosistant](https://www.ecosistant.eu/en/services/packaging-authorized-representative/)
- Amazon has blocked German listings that lack a LUCID number since 2022. A September 2026 analysis says enforcement in practice happens mainly in Germany. — [expan.do](https://www.expan.do/post/ppwr-marketplaces-authorised-representative-enforcement)
- A WEEE registration that lapsed led Amazon to flag listings as non-compliant and remove them. This is a vendor anecdote. — [VATAi](https://www.vatai.com/blog/epr-renewal-guide-2025)
- Etsy has reportedly begun checks for sellers shipping to France and Germany. This is a vendor claim; I found no Etsy policy text. — [avask](https://avask.com/epr-eu-packaging-rules/)
- In the Netherlands, Verpact suspended registrations from non-EU entities, and Poland's BDO register has about a 6-month wait. — [expan.do](https://www.expan.do/post/ppwr-marketplaces-authorised-representative-enforcement)

**European Accessibility Act (EAA)**
- The EAA has applied since 28 Jun 2025. Existing services have a transition period to 28 Jun 2030. Microenterprises (under 10 staff and under €2M turnover) are exempt from the service requirements. — [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/understanding-the-european-accessibility-act-risks-of-non-compliance-and-key-authorities); [fanatic.co.uk](https://fanatic.co.uk/blog/eaa-enforcement-whats-happening-now/)
- Fines are set nationally and sources conflict:
  - France: maximum €7,500 for legal entities, per Fieldfisher.
  - Kolonell (a vendor) claims up to €50,000 per service.
  - Spain: up to €1M, per Clym.
  - Enforcement is early stage, and regulators typically give 30–90 days to fix issues. — [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en/insights/understanding-the-european-accessibility-act-risks-of-non-compliance-and-key-authorities); [Clym](https://www.clym.io/blog/european-accessibility-act-fines-by-country); [kolonell](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/eaa-accessibility-fines-remediation-budget-sme-site-2026)
- Price benchmarks, all from one vendor (Kolonell):
  - WCAG audit: €1,500–8,000.
  - Fixes: €3,000–30,000.
  - Accessibility statement: €300–1,200.
  - Re-audit: €700–3,500.
  - Source: [kolonell cost guide](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/web-accessibility-compliance-cost-wcag-2026); [kolonell SME audit](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/eaa-accessibility-audit-and-fixes-budget-sme-2026)
- Regulators are expected to assess against EN 301 549 (WCAG 2.1 AA). — [fanatic.co.uk](https://fanatic.co.uk/blog/eaa-enforcement-whats-happening-now/)

**Belgium: B2B e-invoicing over Peppol**
- Mandatory since 1 Jan 2026 for domestic B2B transactions between VAT-registered companies. Non-established businesses are excluded. Fines run from 1 Apr 2026: €1,500 for a first breach, €3,000 for a second, €5,000 for later ones. Sources conflict on exemptions (flat-rate taxpayers until 2028, among others). — [sharedserviceslink](https://www.sharedserviceslink.com/news/belgium-mandate-grace-period-ends); [accountable.eu](https://help.accountable.eu/en/articles/12105986-mandatory-peppol-invoicing-fines-and-penalties-for-businesses); [Deloitte BE](https://www.deloitte.com/be/en/services/tax/blogs/tax-legal-pulse/belgium-peppol-mandate-what-changes-for-smes.html); [Billdu](https://www.billdu.com/blog/belgium-e-invoicing-mandate/)

**UK: Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD ITSA)**
- From 6 Apr 2026, sole traders and landlords with qualifying gross income over £50k must comply. HMRC estimates more than 860,000 people; Price Bailey estimates about 780k. The threshold drops to £30k in Apr 2027 and £20k in Apr 2028. — [Inverclyde Chamber](https://inverclydechamber.co.uk/act-now-864000-sole-traders-and-landlords-face-new-tax-rules-in-two-months/); [Price Bailey](https://www.pricebailey.co.uk/guides/mtd-guide/); [Lloyds](https://www.lloydsbank.com/business/resource-centre/business-guides/making-tax-digital.html); [Deloitte](https://taxscape.deloitte.com/measures-spring-statement-2025/making-tax-digital.aspx)
- Mishcon calls it "five filings a year": quarterly updates plus a final declaration. — [Mishcon](https://www.mishcon.com/news/five-filings-a-year-are-you-ready-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax)
- About 40% of those affected reportedly have no accountant. This comes from a search-result summary of [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/what-making-tax-digital-software-should-you-use-arBDz0t9yNfX) and was not checked against HMRC.
- Bridging software reads Excel records and submits them to HMRC. — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/money/tax/income-tax/making-tax-digital-mtd-for-income-tax-aboJ39E1tbWw)
- Affiliate precedent in UK tax: Taxfix pays £25 per sale. — [Taxfix](https://taxfix.com/en-uk/?p=28557)

**UK: Companies House identity verification**
- Anyone filing on behalf of a company must be a registered Authorised Corporate Service Provider (ACSP). That requires AML supervision and a £55 fee. — [ICAS](https://icas.com/news-insights-events/news/practice/what-you-need-to-know-about-becoming-an-authorised-corporate-service-provider); [Bright](https://brightsg.com/blog/accountants-companies-house-identity-verification-2025/)
- Vendor prices: Icon Offices from £24.99 per person; 1office £99, or £349 plus VAT for a manual check. — [1office](https://1office.co/uk/services/identity-verification/); [newsfilecorp release](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/310637)

### Inferences
- **GPSR and EPR suit this owner best.** The pain is binary and costly (blocked listings or stopped sales). Delivery is a contract with a licensed EU provider, so no face or labour is needed. Buyers are B2B and global, including Brazilian, US, UK and Chinese sellers. The owner cannot legally be the RP or authorised representative himself without an EU entity, so the model is reseller or white-label: a partner such as EAS or ecosistant acts as RP/AR, and the owner sells a bundle on top (onboarding, document pack, label templates, a compliance checklist, and the LUCID registration walkthrough). An affiliate or referral deal is the minimum version.
- **MTD ITSA: affiliate, not service.** The owner is not UK-based and cannot act as a tax agent without UK credentials. A safe offer is a Google Sheets/Excel MTD record-keeping template paired with an affiliate link to HMRC-recognised bridging software. The audience is large (860k now, millions after 2027–28) and price-sensitive.
- **Belgium Peppol: affiliate or reseller of access-point software.** Doing the setup as a service is possible, but Belgium is a small, multilingual market (FR/NL).
- **EAA accessibility: a productized audit plus accessibility statement can work, with three caveats.** Delivery needs real WCAG auditors (freelancers on Upwork, or EU partners). Microenterprises are exempt, which shrinks the market. Enforcement is soft, which weakens urgency. Do not sell overlay widgets as "compliance"; this is a reputational and legal risk (no source retrieved; see Gaps).
- **Companies House IDV: avoid as a direct service.** It needs ACSP status plus AML supervision, and Google's government-services ad policy probably catches it (see Q3).

## Q2. Expat and immigrant document pains (Portugal NIF, AIMA, nationality, driving-licence exchange; Spain NIE; Italy citizenship): what can be sold done-for-you without being a lawyer?

### Takeaway
The demand is huge and Portuguese-speaking, but it is legally and advertising-wise the most dangerous area. Portugal criminalises procuradoria ilícita (unlawful practice of acts reserved to lawyers and solicitors), including advertising those acts. Courts have convicted a non-lawyer who held powers of attorney for Brazilian nationality filings. Google also restricts ads for help obtaining government documents. Viable versions: a lead-generation or white-label partnership with a registered solicitador or advogado, information products, or affiliate links to existing NIF providers. Even these face policy friction.

### Cited Findings
- **NIF services.** The online route for non-residents is via a tax representative. Services cost about €64–150: Visas.pt €64, Novomove €69 including 1 year of representation, Anchorless €89, Bordr about $140. — [ePortugal official](https://eportugal.gov.pt/en/servicos/pedir-o-numero-de-identificacao-fiscal-para-pessoa-singular); [Portugalist (affiliate comparison)](https://www.portugalist.com/nif-number-portugal/); [idealista 2026](https://idealista.pt/en/news/legal-advice-in-portugal/2026/01/27/73478-how-to-get-a-nif-in-portugal-a-complete-guide-for-expats-and-foreigners)
  - Portugalist is itself an affiliate of these NIF providers, which proves an affiliate model exists.
- **Law on reserved acts.** Lei 49/2004 was revoked and replaced by Lei 10/2024 (Regime Jurídico dos Atos de Advogados e Solicitadores). Acts reserved to lawyers and solicitors may only be performed by registered professionals. — [Ordem dos Advogados](https://portal.oa.pt/ordem/regras-profissionais/regime-juridico-dos-atos-de-advogados-e-solicitadores-lei-n%C2%BA-102024-de-19-de-janeiro/lei-dos-actos-proprios-dos-advogados-lei-n%C2%BA-492004-de-24-de-agosto-revogado/); [Diário da República (Lei 49/2004)](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/49-2004-479604)
- **Case law.**
  - The Coimbra Court of Appeal (2020) upheld a procuradoria ilícita finding against a person holding at least 11 powers of attorney from Brazilian citizens for nationality applications. This held even though the nationality law does not require a lawyer for those filings. — [TRC 2020](https://trc.pt/atos-proprios-dos-advogados-e-dos-solicitadores-mandato-forense-crime-de-procuradoria-ilicita-atos-proprios-de-advogados-e-solicitadores-sua-pratica-reiterada-ou-sua-pratica-com-caracter-remunerado-pr/); [vLex](https://vlex.pt/vid/acordao-n-78-18-1044231045)
  - The Lisbon Court of Appeal (2025) extends the offence to mere advertising of reserved acts. — [vLex TRL 2025](https://vlex.pt/vid/acordao-n-18190-21-1079092534)
- **Nationality law change.** Lei Orgânica 1/2026 has been in force since 19 May 2026. Naturalisation now needs 10 years of residence instead of 5 (7 for EU/CPLP citizens). Pending applications stay under the old law; the Constitutional Court ruled partially unconstitutional in Acórdão 409/2026. — [ECO](https://eco.sapo.pt/descodificador/o-que-muda-com-a-lei-da-nacionalidade/01-o-que-e-a-a-nova-lei-da-nacionalidade/); [Sovereign Group](https://www.sovereigngroup.com/news/portugal-tightens-naturalisation-rules-doubles-residency-requirement/); [MCS](https://mcs.pt/pt/Atualiza%C3%A7%C3%B5es-da-lei-de-nacionalidade-portuguesa-em-2026%3A-uma-an%C3%A1lise-jur%C3%ADdica-para-expatriados-na-Madeira-e-em-Portugal./)
  - Unofficial backlog figures: 522k active nationality requests at IRN (mid-2025), or more than 700k accumulated. These come from consultancy sources. — [cidadaniaevisto](https://www.cidadaniaevisto.com.br/blog/queixas-sobre-nacionalidade-portuguesa-crescem-177-atrasos-superiores-a-4-anos-e)
- **Driving-licence exchange (troca de carta).**
  - One user reports paying €27 for an online request to exchange a Brazilian licence. Delays of up to about 15 months appear in complaints. — [Portal da Queixa](https://portaldaqueixa.com/brands/imtt-instituto-da-mobilidade-e-dos-transportes-terrestres/complaints/imt-imt-coimbra-troca-de-carta-de-conducao-pendente-ha-15-meses-164189226)
  - Brazilian licences with a QR code (issued from 18 May 2017) can be exchanged directly on the IMT platform without a consular authenticity declaration (IMT, 2022). — [IMT](https://www.imt-ip.pt/noticias/brasil-disponibiliza-tecnologia-para-confirmacao-dos-seus-titulos-de-conducao/)
- **Italian citizenship documents (sold mostly to Brazilians, searched in Brazil).**
  - Brazil-side document preparation costs about R$10,000 for a small family.
  - Sworn translation costs R$70–150 per page; apostille about R$100 per document.
  - Fixing errors in records costs R$1,500–5,000.
  - Since 2017, full-extract ("inteiro teor") certificates are required.
  - Sources: [Âmbito Jurídico](https://ambitojuridico.com.br/quanto-custa-para-tirar-a-cidadania-italiana/); [Italianismo](https://italianismo.com.br/quase-2-milhoes-de-documentos-ja-foram-apostilados-pelos-cartorios-no-brasil/)

### Inferences
- **Portugal immigration done-for-you by a non-lawyer is high risk.** Signing powers of attorney before AIMA or IRN, or advertising such acts, risks criminal procuradoria ilícita. The only compliant model is a registered solicitador or advogado as the service provider, with the owner as marketer or lead source.
  - Even here, the Ordem dos Advogados' rules on advertising and on soliciting clients limit paying per lead or sharing fees. A flat marketing fee or white-label tech arrangement needs local legal advice.
- **Low-risk Portugal plays:**
  - (a) Affiliate links to NIF providers (Bordr, Anchorless and others have affiliate programs, as Portugalist shows).
  - (b) Paid guides or checklists in PT-BR, such as "troca de carta passo a passo" or "documentos para nacionalidade". This is an information product, not representation.
  - (c) A document-retrieval service for Brazilian civil records (certidões, apostille, sworn translation). These are acts performed by Brazilian cartórios and sworn translators, not legal acts.
  - Volume is large because Brazilians are the largest immigrant community in Portugal (background knowledge; not re-verified this session).
- **Italian citizenship documents** have the same structure: a document concierge in Brazil, outsourced to despachantes and translators. The market is in Brazil rather than Europe, and it shrank after Italy's 2025 jure sanguinis restriction (Decree-Law 36/2025, converted into Law 74/2025, limiting it to about 2 generations). That last point is background knowledge, not verified in this session. Flag it as declining.
- **Spain NIE:** not researched this session. It probably carries the same Google government-documents policy problem.

### Gaps
- No verified search volume or CPC. EST: "troca de carta brasileira Portugal" and "NIF Portugal online" are probably 1–10k per month combined from Brazil and Portugal, with CPC €0.5–2.5 (unverified).
- The Portuguese legal-services CPC benchmark (€2.50–7, over €10 in Lisbon) comes from a search summary of [Marketeer/SAPO](https://marketeer.sapo.pt/quanto-custa-um-clique-em-portugal-advogados-pagam-ate-35-vezes-mais-do-que-restaurantes/). The fetch failed, so the date and data source are unconfirmed.
- I did not verify the text of Lei 10/2024 or the OA's advertising and fee-sharing rules.
- Spain's NIE rules and Spanish legal limits (gestor administrativo vs abogado) were not researched.

## Q3. Google Ads policy, CPC and saturation: which ideas can actually run on Search?

### Takeaway
Google's "Government documents and official services" policy is the main blocker for anything that helps people obtain NIF, NIE, driving licences, residence permits, Companies House filings and similar. Only governments and certified, government-authorised providers may advertise "direct acquisition", and a reported October 2026 tightening raises the bar further. Product-compliance services (GPSR RP, EPR authorised representative, accessibility audits) and software affiliates are not government documents, so they face ordinary policy.

### Cited Findings
- **Core rule.** Only certified governments and authorised providers may run ads that promote direct acquisition of government documents and services. Advertisers must also be certified by Google. — [Google Ads policy 12921812](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/12921812?hl=en-GB)
  - The fetch failed on DNS; this is a search summary of the page.
- **History.** The 2020 policy banned ads for documents or services obtainable directly from government, "including offers of assistance to obtain these". In 2023 it opened to "government authorised providers": the provider's domain must be linked from an official government website and named as authorised. — [Google policy 9736337](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9736337); [SEJ](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-ads-introduces-new-policies-for-government-documents-services/356440); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-releases-video-guide-on-government-document-advertising-policy/)
- **October 2026 tightening (single secondary source; fetch failed).** From 5 Oct 2026:
  - Commercial contracts, business licences and registry entries no longer count as proof of authorisation.
  - Ads must target only the region the authorisation covers.
  - Exceptions exist for e-travel visas and similar.
  - Source: [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/google-ads-en/google-ads-government-documents-services-policy-october-2026/)
- **CPC benchmarks.** Portugal legal and financial €2.50–7, over €10 in Lisbon (search summary; fetch failed). Global attorneys $8.58, overall average $5.42 (WordStream 2025). — [Marketeer/SAPO](https://marketeer.sapo.pt/quanto-custa-um-clique-em-portugal-advogados-pagam-ate-35-vezes-mais-do-que-restaurantes/); [Shopifreaks/WordStream](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/)
- **Competitor density (proves demand).**
  - GPSR: EAS, euverify, Webinterpret, VATAi and Fivex are visible.
  - EPR: ecosistant, avask, ecocomply.ai, VATAi.
  - EAA: many agencies, including Kolonell with dozens of city-specific SEO pages.
  - NIF: at least 5 providers plus affiliate comparison sites.
  - Sources: the links above; [ecocomply](https://ecocomply.ai/epr-registration)

### Inferences
- **Government documents policy:** NIF/NIE, troca de carta, AIMA scheduling and Companies House IDV landing pages that sell help obtaining them will likely be disapproved. Pure information guides might pass if they don't sell acquisition, but that is a grey area with suspension risk to the owner's whole Google Ads account. This matters because he runs another business: use a separate account and entity.
- **Legal services:** Google has no general certification requirement for legal services in the EU (background knowledge, unverified). Portugal's procuradoria ilícita risk is a legal issue, not a Google one.
- **Saturation:**
  - GDPR/cookie banners: oversaturated (CMP SaaS plus free plugins).
  - EAA in DE/NL: getting crowded with agencies.
  - GPSR RP: competitive but fragmented, and it has a clear niche: Portuguese-language sellers (Brazilian sellers going to the EU) and small Etsy/Shopify sellers.
  - MTD: big brands (Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent) bid on head terms. Long-tail terms such as "MTD spreadsheet template" or "bridging software for sole traders" are more viable (EST).

### Gaps
- Google's official October 2026 policy text was not read directly.
- No Keyword Planner access, so all volume and CPC figures are EST.
- No Google Transparency Center check of who is currently advertising on these keywords.
