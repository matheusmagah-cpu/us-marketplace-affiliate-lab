# USA: high-pain, low-touch opportunities sold via Google Search Ads (remote, faceless, Brazilian owner)

Research date: 2026-10-25. Legend: **[V]** = verified by a cited source (often a vendor/secondary source, noted as such); **[E]** = analyst estimate with no public source found; validate in Google Keyword Planner before spending. Note: support.google.com could not be fetched from this environment, so Google Ads policy details come from secondary summaries (Search Engine Land, Relevant Audience, TechWyse) plus the policy page title seen in search results.

## Q1. Regulatory status check and which niches to exclude now

### Takeaway
BOI filing as a service is no longer viable for US customers: FinCEN made the domestic-company exemption permanent in August 2026. Several other niches on the list are blocked or high-risk because of Google's Government Documents & Services policy (stricter from Oct 5, 2026), scam associations (hacked-account recovery), or state ESA laws. These are noted below so they are not pursued.

### Cited Findings
- **BOI is dead for domestic entities.** FinCEN's final rule (RIN 1506-AB67), issued Aug 11, 2026 and effective Aug 14, 2026, permanently adopts the March 2025 interim final rule. US companies and US beneficial owners do not report; only foreign entities registered in the US still report, and only for non-US beneficial owners — [Ballard Spahr](https://www.ballardspahr.com/insights/alerts-and-articles/2026/08/fincen-permanently-exempts-us-entities-and-persons-from-beneficial-ownership-reporting); [Adams and Reese](https://www.adamsandreese.com/insights/fincen-finalizes-exemption-of-us-companies-from-beneficial-ownership-reporting); [Florida Realtors](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/08/fincen-finalizes-reporting-exemption-us-businesses). (One source dates the interim rule to March 2026, which conflicts with the others: [taxesforexpats](https://cdn.taxesforexpats.com/articles/news/new-beneficial-ownership-information-reporting-for-small-business-owners.html).) [V]
- **Google Ads Government documents & services policy.** Only certified governments and "authorized providers" may advertise direct acquisition of specific government documents and services. An authorized provider's domain must be linked from an official government website that explicitly names it as authorized — [Google policy page 13156083 (title/snippet via search)](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/13156083); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-tightens-ads-policy-for-government-document-providers-484342). [V, secondary]
- **Stricter from Oct 5, 2026.** Commercial contracts, business/trade licenses, company registrations, and blog posts on government sites no longer count as proof of authorization. Ads must stay within the region the authorization covers. There are exemptions for e-travel visas and authorizations, border-entry documents and US Trusted Traveler programs — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/google-ads-en/google-ads-government-documents-services-policy-october-2026/); [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/google-ads-government-documents-services-policy-update-october-2026). [V, secondary]
- **Since Oct 2025, third-party ads in this category must disclose that they are not the government** — [Kliken help article](https://help.kliken.com/en/articles/12461228-google-ads-policy-update-government-documents-and-services-disclosure-october-2025). [V, secondary]
- **History of trucking-registration scams.** In 2016 the FTC charged operators who posed as USDOT to sell carrier registrations. They took in more than $19M, charged hidden service fees of $25–$550+, and auto-enrolled carriers in UCR renewals — [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2016/10/ftc-charges-operators-scheme-used-fake-government-affiliation-sell-commercial-trucking-registration). FMCSA runs a fraud-alerts page and has warned about fake Motus portals; the MCS-150 update is free on FMCSA's site — [FMCSA Fraud Alerts](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/fraud-alerts); [FreightWaves](https://freightwaves.com/news/fmcsa-warns-trucking-companies-about-4-fake-motus-registration-sites). [V]
- **Hacked social account "recovery services" are widely treated as a scam category.** Official recovery (instagram.com/hacked) is free, and security vendors warn that paid recovery helpers often steal accounts a second time — [Bitdefender](https://www.bitdefender.com/en-gb/blog/hotforsecurity/instagram-account-recovery-scams); [ESET](https://eset.com/blog/en/home-topics/privacy-and-identity-protection/recover-hacked-instagram-account/); [Guard.io](https://guard.io/blog/instagram-account-hacked-recovery). [V]
- **ESA letters.** California AB 468 requires a 30-day client-provider relationship before a licensed professional can issue an ESA letter, with penalties for fraudulent letters. Sources say Arkansas, Iowa, Louisiana and Montana have similar rules — [TherapyTrainings](https://www.therapytrainings.com/pages/blog/california-esa-letter); [Eduyush](https://eduyush.com/blogs/formats-and-templates/who-can-sign-an-esa-letter) (vendor blogs; the statute text was not retrieved). [V, weak]

### Inferences
- **Exclude:** BOI filing (no demand). Expedited passport, vital records and apostille ads that promise to obtain the document (gov-docs policy; certification is practically impossible for a private reseller after Oct 2026). Hacked-account recovery (scam reputation, likely ad disapprovals, chargebacks). ESA letters (health plus licensed-clinician requirement, state laws, Google healthcare restrictions, reputational risk). Credit repair (heavily regulated by CROA and state laws; excluded per brief). HOA/tenant demand letters and small-claims document prep written for the customer's specific facts (unauthorized practice of law risk). Only self-help templates sold as general digital products are defensible there. Negative content/reputation "removal" services (high refund/scam perception; Google generally does not remove lawful content; risk of misleading claims).
- **Trucking filings (USDOT/MC/UCR) are high-risk for Google Ads** because they look like government services and the category has a documented scam history. BOC-3 is technically a private process-agent service, which may make it less exposed, but it is usually sold alongside USDOT/MC filings.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the full Google restricted-documents list to confirm whether apostille, USDOT/MC, UCR and BOC-3 ads fall in scope. Run a test ad or ask Google support before investing.
- Google's current treatment of ESA advertising (healthcare/unapproved-claims rules) was not confirmed.

## Q2. Shortlist of 7 US opportunities with scores (1-5)

### Takeaway
The strongest fits for a remote, faceless owner with mostly ad spend are B2B compliance and recovery services where (a) pain is lawsuit- or revenue-driven, (b) delivery can be outsourced to freelancers or white-label partners, and (c) the service is not a government document. The best are ADA website accessibility remediation, sales-tax compliance for ecommerce sellers, and marketplace suspension appeals. Brand-impersonation/DMCA takedown subscriptions and pay-per-call emergency restoration lead gen are viable but harder. Trucking compliance and apostille coordination are scored but flagged red for ad policy.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. ADA/WCAG website accessibility audit + remediation (productized, freelance devs)
- **Pain:** UsableNet counted 2,019 digital accessibility suits in H1 2025 and more than 5,000 for 2025 overall (tracker figure 4,928). eCommerce was the target in 69% of cases, and 1,427 suits hit repeat defendants — [Newswire / UsableNet](https://www.newswire.com/news/digital-accessibility-lawsuits-surge-in-2025-22618502). Seyfarth counts 3,117 federal website suits in 2025, up 27% from 2,452 — per the same search summary (Seyfarth dataset). [V]
- **Overlays don't solve the problem:** In Jan 2025 the FTC announced a proposed $1M settlement with accessiBe over claims that its AI widget makes sites WCAG compliant; the final order followed in April 2025 — [Hudson Cook](https://www.hudsoncook.com/article/ftc-targets-accessibility-overlays/). A vendor claims 983 suits in 2025 targeted sites that had widgets installed — [TestParty](https://testparty.ai/blog/sued-with-accessibe-installed) (competitor source). [V]
- **Prices:** accessiBe widget about $490–$3,990/yr. Manual audit tiers about $479/mo. Agency flat audit about $5,000 — [A11y Collective](https://a11y-collective.com/blog/accessibility-overlays/); [webability](https://www.webability.io/compare/accessibe-alternative) (vendor-reported). Settlements are commonly quoted at $5k–$20k+ before defense fees — [accessibilitychecker.org](https://www.accessibilitychecker.org/blog/ada-website-compliance-lawsuits/) (unverified). [V, vendor]
- **Search:** "ada compliance" about 16,510/mo, CPC about $3.39. "ada site compliance" 6,600/mo, $15.63. "american with disabilities act website compliance" 6,600/mo, $29.70 — [seodata.dev](https://www.seodata.dev/keyword/accessibility-standards-ada); [indexator.ai](https://indexator.ai/companies/accessibe) (third-party tool estimates). [V, tool estimates]
- **Scores:** Pain 5 (lawsuits and demand letters; "received ADA demand letter" is the hottest intent). Deliverable 4 (manual audit plus code fixes on Shopify/WordPress does fix the issues; no one can guarantee immunity from suits). Hands-off 4 (WCAG auditors and devs on Upwork; checklists and automated scanners such as axe or WAVE for pre-sales). Faceless 5. Search 4 (moderate volume; commercial CPCs $3–$30 [V tool]). Economics/risk 4 (sell at $1.5k–$5k per site plus a $99–$299/mo monitoring retainer [E]; freelance cost roughly 30–40% [E]. No gov-docs policy issue. Do not claim "lawsuit-proof" (FTC deceptive-claims precedent). Do not give legal advice on lawsuits; refer defense to attorneys). **Total about 26/30.**

#### 2. Sales tax nexus registration + filing for ecommerce/Amazon sellers (done-for-you via US preparer partners or API tools)
- **Prices:** Numeral charges $75 per filing, $150 per registration and $150 per backfiling, with no subscription — [Numeral pricing](https://www.numeralhq.com/pricing). TaxValet is managed at about $560/mo entry ([G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/taxvalet-vs-vertex-inc-vertex-vs-sovos)), and a competitor says most TaxValet customers pay $1k–$3k/mo ([Numeral comparison](https://www.numeral.com/taxvalet-alternative)). Low-cost filers: Tax Rex $20/filing, Sidr $35–$45, Taxwire $100/return — [Kintsugi blog](https://trykintsugi.com/blog/best-sales-tax-software.md); [TaxCloud](https://taxcloud.com/blog/numeral-sales-tax-alternatives/). [V, vendor]
- **Scores:** Pain 4 (penalties, back taxes and marketplace notices; less urgent until a state letter arrives). Deliverable 4. Hands-off 4 (wholesale filing via $20–$45/filing tools plus a VA; resell at $75–$150/filing and $150–$300/registration — about 50–70% gross margin [E]). Faceless 5. Search 3 ("sales tax filing service", "economic nexus" etc. — probably low thousands per month with B2B CPC around $10–$30 [E, no source found]). Risk 4 (state registration is done by the seller or a third-party filer under POA, which is a common practice. Positioned as "compliance service", not gov-docs. Avoid income-tax advice; check whether the states you file in require a registered tax preparer). **Total about 24/30.**

#### 3. Marketplace / ad account suspension appeals (Amazon, Walmart, Etsy, Meta ad accounts)
- **Prices:** Amazon appeals cost $1,500 flat ($2,300 for related-account or IP appeals) at AMZ Sellers Attorney — [amazonsellers.attorney](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/best-amazon-appeal-services.html); $2,000 per account elsewhere; $3,500 flat at another firm; consultants about $500–$3,000; prevention subscriptions $500/mo — [SellerEngine](https://sellerengine.com/services/account-rescue/); [terms.law](https://terms.law/Demand-Letters/Online-Marketplace/amazon-seller-account-suspension-demand.html). [V, vendor]
- **Scores:** Pain 5 (revenue frozen; sellers search right away). Deliverable 3 (a good plan of action helps but Amazon decides; vendor success claims of 70–90% are unverified). Hands-off 3 (needs experienced POA writers, who can be ex-Amazon freelancers, plus case intake; more owner QA than the others). Faceless 5. Search 3 ("amazon account suspended", "amazon appeal service": medium volume, competitive, CPC probably $5–$20 [E]). Economics/risk 4 (price $500–$1,500, writer cost $150–$400 [E]. Need clear no-guarantee terms to limit chargebacks. Do not present non-attorney work as legal representation; IP/counterfeit cases can verge on legal advice). **Total about 23/30.**

#### 4. Brand impersonation / DMCA takedown subscription (software-lite plus VA)
- **Prices:** Ceartas $69/$169/$349 per month; LeakRemover from $99/mo; Takedown.co from $39 per stage name/mo; Lawyerd lawyer-backed retainer — [Ceartas list](https://www.ceartas.io/blog/best-impersonation-removal-services); [Capterra LeakRemover](https://www.capterra.com/p/10050771/LeakRemover/). [V, vendor]
- **Scores:** Pain 4 (leaked creator content and fake brand stores/accounts; recurring). Deliverable 3 (re-uploads happen; LeakRemover itself says permanent removal is not guaranteed). Hands-off 4 (monitoring tools plus a VA sending templated DMCA notices). Faceless 5. Search 2–3 ("dmca takedown service", "remove leaked content": modest volume [E]). Risk 3 (much of the market is adult creators, which raises Google Ads sexual-content restrictions and payment-processor risk; filing a DMCA notice on behalf of a rights holder as an agent is common, but false notices carry liability under 17 USC 512(f)). Recurring revenue is attractive. **Total about 21/30.**

#### 5. Pay-per-call / rank-and-rent for emergency water damage (also mold, plumbing)
- **Economics:** Exclusive water-damage calls sell for about $300–$600 in competitive markets, and exclusive web leads up to $700 — [Elevarus](https://elevarus.com/water-damage-restoration-leads/); [leadgen-economy](https://www.leadgen-economy.com/blog/water-damage-restoration-emergency-leads-guide/). Search-ads cost per lead runs $300–$400 in mid-to-large markets; average cost per call $82 (top accounts $38); LSA cost per lead averages $156 (range $83–$390); typical job $3k–$7k — [WebTonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/water-damage-restoration-google-ads-statistics) (agency compilation). Plumbing search CPL $129 (LocaliQ 2025, secondhand) — [WebTonic plumbing](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/plumbing-seo-statistics). WordStream 2025 Home & Home Improvement CPC $7.85 — [Shopifreaks summary](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/). [V, vendor/secondary]
- **Competition:** LSAs sit above text ads for many plumbing/emergency queries — [WebTonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/plumbing-seo-statistics); [CI Web Group](https://www.ciwebgroup.com/blog/plumbing-google-ads). [V, weak]
- **Scores:** Pain 5. Deliverable 4 (you route the call; a licensed partner does the work). Hands-off 3 (call tracking, buyer management and disputes over call quality; buyers churn). Faceless 5. Search 4 (high volume, but CPCs for "water damage restoration near me" are commonly $30–$100+ [E]). Economics/risk 2 (margin is thin: at $82 cost per call and about $300 sale price it works only if most calls are billable. Google's ad-verification rules for local services and its scrutiny of lead-gen sites posing as local providers (misrepresentation) are a risk. LSAs are reserved for verified, licensed businesses, so a lead generator cannot use them). **Total about 23/30, but it is the most capital-intensive and has the highest variance.**

#### 6. Trucking new-authority compliance bundle (BOC-3 via white-label process agent + UCR/MCS-150 reminders)
- **Prices:** BOC-3 costs about $25–$125 one-time, $20–$55 plus forwarding fees, or $19–$35/yr from high-volume agents — [O Trucking](https://otrucking.com/resources/guides/boc-3-cost/); [CorpNet](https://www.corpnet.com/?p=67285); [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/ghost-agents-running-americas-trucking-legal-infrastructure). Only a process agent company can file the BOC-3 for a carrier; operating authority is not activated without it — [Foley](https://www.foleyservices.com/articles/boc-3-form-filing/). [V]
- **Scores:** Pain 4 (cannot haul without active authority). Deliverable 4. Hands-off 4 (resell a process agent's service). Faceless 5. Search 3 ("boc-3 filing", "ucr registration": seasonal spikes Oct–Jan [E]). Risk 1 (Google gov-docs policy, FTC scam precedent, FMCSA fraud alerts; BOC-3 itself is a commodity at $20–$50). **Total about 21/30. RED for Google Ads.**

#### 7. Apostille coordination (US courier/notary partners)
- **Prices:** State fees about $5–$25 per document. Full-service providers charge $95–$225 per document; Las Vegas coordinators $185–$250 for one certificate, family packets $450–$950+; overnight courier $25–$75 extra — [philly-docs-hub](https://philly-docs-hub.lovable.app/learning-center/how-much-does-an-apostille-cost); [Lake Mead Mobile Notary](https://lakemeadmobilenotary.com/learn/nevada-apostille-pricing-guide-2026). [V, vendor]
- **Scores:** Pain 4 (deadlines for visas, foreign marriages, immigration). Deliverable 4. Hands-off 3 (physical documents; needs a courier partner in each state's capital). Faceless 5. Search 3 [E]. Risk 2 (likely in scope of the gov-docs policy as a "government service"; unconfirmed). **Total about 21/30, YELLOW/RED.**

### Inferences
- **CPC versus price:** ADA remediation ($1.5k–$5k ticket against a $3–$30 CPC), Amazon appeals ($500–$1.5k ticket against an estimated $5–$20 CPC) and sales-tax packages (recurring $75+/filing) can absorb CPCs at plausible 2–5% landing-page conversion [E]. For example, a $15 CPC at 3% conversion means about $500 CAC, which works against a $2,500 ADA job or a recurring filing client. Pay-per-call water damage works only with tight geo and call-quality control, because the CPC-to-sale-price ratio is about 1:4–1:6.
- **Saturation:** Water damage, plumbing, and Amazon appeals are crowded (many agencies and law firms). ADA remediation is crowded by overlay vendors, but post-FTC "manual audit plus real fixes" positioning is differentiated. Sales tax mid-market is served by SaaS; the micro-seller "done-for-you under $100/filing" gap is less served [E].
- **Foreign-owner feasibility:** Stripe Atlas forms Delaware LLCs or C-corps for non-US founders ([Stripe docs](https://docs.stripe.com/atlas)). Foreign-owned US LLCs typically get an EIN via Form SS-4 and bank with Mercury, Relay or Wise ([Zenind](https://www.zenind.com/help/post/how-international-founders-can-create-a-us-stripe-account), vendor). A foreign-owned single-member LLC must also file Form 5472 yearly (general knowledge; not verified in this session).

### Gaps
- Exact search volume and CPC for sales tax, Amazon appeal, DMCA, BOC-3, apostille and water-damage keywords could not be verified (no public Semrush/Ahrefs data found; the Ahrefs/Manifold connectors were unavailable). Values marked [E] must be checked in Google Keyword Planner.
- The Google Ads Transparency Center was not checked to see whether competitors in each niche are actively running ads.
- No reliable source was found for rank-and-rent rental prices or for LSA's share of clicks.
- Google Ads verification requirements for a Brazil-based advertiser running US campaigns, and the treatment of "reputation management" and legal-adjacent ads, were not confirmed.

## Q3. Ranking and recommended starting point

### Takeaway
Rank by fit: (1) ADA/WCAG manual audit + remediation, about 26/30; (2) Sales-tax done-for-you filing for small ecommerce sellers, about 24; (3) Amazon/Walmart suspension appeals, about 23; (4) Pay-per-call water damage, about 23 but capital-heavy; (5) DMCA/impersonation takedown subscription, about 21; (6) Apostille coordination, about 21, policy risk; (7) Trucking BOC-3/UCR, about 21, policy red. Excluded: BOI, ESA letters, hacked-account recovery, credit repair, expedited passports, demand letters and small-claims prep, reputation removal.

### Cited Findings
- Legal services had the highest average CPC in WordStream's 2025 benchmarks ($8.58) and the highest cost per lead ($131.63). The all-industry average CPC was $5.26–$5.42 (sources conflict) and average CPL $70.11 — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/); [Backlinko (Semrush data, Jul 2025: legal median $8, home services median $5.82)](https://backlinko.com/how-much-does-google-ads-cost). [V, secondary]
- The ADA lawsuit volume and repeat-defendant data above support continuing demand — [Newswire / UsableNet](https://www.newswire.com/news/digital-accessibility-lawsuits-surge-in-2025-22618502). [V]

### Inferences
- **Suggested test for #1:** a single landing page "Received an ADA website demand letter? Manual WCAG 2.1 AA audit + fixes for Shopify/WordPress in 10 days" with exact-match keywords ("ada website compliance", "wcag audit", "ada demand letter website"), a $1–2k test budget [E], fulfilment by vetted Upwork WCAG specialists, and an attorney-referral disclaimer.
- **Bundle idea:** pair #1 and #2 under one "ecommerce compliance" brand to cross-sell to the same Shopify/Amazon audience.

### Gaps
- Real conversion rates for these offers are unknown; only small paid tests can establish them.
