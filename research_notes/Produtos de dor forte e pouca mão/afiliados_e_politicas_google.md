# Affiliate programs for urgent-pain products + Google Ads policy and economics constraints (as of Oct 2026)

Research method note: Google's help center (support.google.com), LocaliQ, WordStream and Hotmart pages could not be fetched directly from this environment (DNS/proxy block). Findings below come from search-engine extracts of those pages plus third-party summaries. Where a claim rests on a search extract of an official page, it is marked "(official page, via search extract)". Third-party directories (UpPromote, Lasso, affi.io, Cuelinks etc.) are often outdated and frequently contradict each other; treat their commission numbers as estimates. Forum/blog case studies are labeled ANECDOTAL.

## (A1) Which affiliate programs pay high commissions on urgent-pain products?

### Takeaway
Several urgent-pain programs pay US$50-150+ per sale or 30%+ recurring (LLC formation/registered agent, VPN, identity theft protection, ADA accessibility, website security), but legal/tax brands (LegalZoom, TurboTax) pay low percentages (~15%), and most commission figures outside official pages are unverified and inconsistent between directories.

### Cited Findings
**LLC formation / registered agent**
- Northwest Registered Agent: $100 per registered-agent signup and $150 per business-formation signup; referral fee reversed if customer cancels within first 90 days (official program page + Awin profile) — [Northwest affiliate page](https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/affiliate-program.html); [Awin terms 66946](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/66946)
- ZenBusiness runs its US program on Awin (commission rate not captured in extract) — [Awin ZenBusiness terms](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/102801)

**Legal documents**
- LegalZoom: commonly listed at ~15%, or "$15-$150 per sale depending on service", program on CJ (third-party directories, unverified; one Skimlinks listing shows 0%) — [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/legalzoom/); [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/legalzoom); [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-directory/legalzoom/)
- Rocket Lawyer: commission not captured; see PPC rules in A2 — [FMTC program directory](https://merchanthub.fmtc.co/cp/program_directory/details/m/30962)

**Tax software**
- TurboTax: Intuit's own affiliate page describes "revenue share of up to 15%" on co-branded microsite revenue, signup via affiliate_support@intuit.com; directories list 10-15% with 15- or 30-day cookies (conflicting) — [Intuit TurboTax affiliate page](http://affiliates.turbotax.intuit.com/public_interface.html?pi=2); [Lasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/turbotax/)

**VPN / identity theft**
- NordVPN: third-party sources say 40%-100% of first sale (100% on monthly plans) and 30% on renewals; Awin terms say commissions are cancelled/deducted on chargebacks, refunds or cancellations — [UpPromote](https://uppromote.com/affiliate-program-directory/nordvpn/); [Awin NordVPN US & CA terms](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/15132); [Creator Hero review](https://www.creator-hero.com/blog/nordvpn-affiliate-program-in-depth-review-pros-and-cons)
- Aura (identity theft protection): most listings give flat $65 CPA with 60-day cookie via Impact; others claim tiered $75-$125/sale or 10% (conflicting, unverified) — [Lasso Aura](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/aura/); [TapRefer](https://taprefer.com/aura-affiliate-program/aura/); [AffNext](https://www.affnext.com/blog/aura-affiliate-program)

**Website security / malware removal**
- Sucuri official referral page: "No we do not offer recurring commissions at this time"; referred customers must stay subscribed 65 days before payout. Third-party directories claim "up to 25%" or "$200 per sale" (conflicting with each other) — [Sucuri referral page](https://sucuri.net/referral/); [Lasso Sucuri](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/sucuri/); [PostAffiliatePro directory](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/sucuri-affiliate-program)

**ADA / web accessibility**
- accessiBe: PartnerStack listing says "up to 30% recurring commission"; accessiBe's own page only says "high commission" — [PartnerStack accessiBe](https://market.partnerstack.com/page/accessibe); [accessiBe affiliates](https://accessibe.com/affiliates)

**ClickBank (info products, many pain niches)**
- ClickBank's own 2026 traffic-source data (last 90 days of offers): Google Ads ranks 6th as a traffic source, with 1.34% hop conversion rate, affiliate EPC $0.80, average payout $67.19 — [ClickBank blog: best traffic sources](https://www.clickbank.com/blog/best-traffic-sources-for-affiliate-marketing)

### Inferences
- The best fixed-payout economics among urgent-pain US offers in this sample are LLC/registered agent (Northwest $100-150) and identity theft/VPN ($65+ or high first-sale %). Legal-doc and tax brands pay ~15% on relatively low tickets, which is thin against $5-9 CPCs.
- Recurring SaaS (accessiBe 30% recurring) has the best LTV but is B2B with long consideration, and a single-click Google Ads funnel recoups slowly.

### Gaps
- No verified data found for: UserWay, Rocket Lawyer commission, ZenBusiness commission rate, data-recovery software (EaseUS/Wondershare/Stellar), home warranty, pest control, background checks (e.g., TruthFinder/BeenVerified), accounting software (QuickBooks/FreshBooks), Kiwify/Eduzz specific programs. Searches returned nothing usable for these within the tool budget.
- Official commission pages on Impact/CJ/PartnerStack require login; directory figures could not be confirmed.

## (A2) Which programs allow or prohibit affiliates bidding on Google Search?

### Takeaway
Most big-brand urgent-pain programs prohibit brand-term bidding and often restrict any PPC without written approval; only fewer than ~15% of programs allow unrestricted brand bidding. Hotmart lets each producer set the rule, and Hotmart's own blog warns that some producers forbid using the product name in Google Ads.

### Cited Findings
- ZenBusiness (Awin terms): affiliates may NOT bid on keywords containing ZenBusiness marks, names or promo materials; any other PPC promoting its links requires written approval; PPC violations are grounds for action — [Awin ZenBusiness terms](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/102801)
- Rocket Lawyer: network directory lists "PPC Bidding Allowed: No" and "PPC Trademark Bidding Allowed: No" (listing date unconfirmed) — [FMTC directory](https://merchanthub.fmtc.co/cp/program_directory/details/m/30962)
- LegalZoom: a third-party page quotes program terms as "no commissions originating from coupon sites, paid ads, discount sites, and other non-first-hand sources are allowed", meaning paid-ad traffic would not earn commission (third-party, unverified) — [affi.io LegalZoom](https://affi.io/m/legalzoom)
- NordVPN: Awin-style directory says PPC generally restricted; Cuelinks listing for NordVPN CPC campaign lists "Disallowed mediums: PPC, SEM, Adult, Gambling, Google ads" (while also mentioning brand keywords; contradictory, so treat as prohibited unless confirmed in writing) — [Cuelinks NordVPN](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/nordvpn-affiliate-program); [Awin NordVPN](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/15132)
- Northwest Registered Agent: no PPC rule found in available pages — [Awin 66946](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/66946)
- Industry base rate: a 2022 analysis cited by The Search Monitor found unrestricted brand bidding was the stated policy in fewer than 15% of active programs — [The Search Monitor](https://thesearchmonitor.com/?p=1307)
- Google itself does not prohibit trademark keywords as keywords; brand-bidding bans are contractual program rules — [MakeInfluence explainer](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/brand-bidding-restrictions-in-affiliate-programs-what-no-branded-keyword-bidding-means)
- Google's Unfair Advantage policy lists "affiliates advertising against their program rules" as a violation, so breaking a program's PPC rule is also a Google policy risk, not only a commission risk (official page, via search extract) — [Google Ads Unfair advantage policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6020954?hl=en)
- Hotmart: official Hotmart blog says to read the producer's rules because "alguns proíbem anunciar o nome do produto no Google Ads", and suggests an intermediate landing page — [Hotmart blog: Google Ads para afiliados](https://hotmart.com/pt-br/blog/google-ads-para-afiliados); [Hotmart blog: divulgar link de afiliado](https://hotmart.com/pt-br/blog/divulgar-link-de-afiliado)
- Brazil legal context: the STJ has repeatedly held that buying a competitor's brand as a Google Ads keyword is, as a rule, unfair competition (same market, risk to brand functions); TJ-SP upheld Google's liability in the Ticket360 case (Sept 2025) — [Migalhas: Performance Max](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/401905/uso-da-campanha-performance-max-no-google-adwords); [Migalhas: uso indevido de marcas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/440327/uso-indevido-de-marcas-como-palavras-chaves-de-anuncios-no-google)

### Inferences
- For a pure affiliate, the highest-intent queries (brand + "coupon", "login", "pricing") are almost always off-limits. What's left is generic pain queries ("form LLC in Texas", "remove malware from WordPress"), where the affiliate competes with the merchant itself and with lead-gen sites for the same clicks.
- In Brazil, bidding on another producer's product name carries contractual risk and also a real legal risk under STJ case law.

### Gaps
- No primary-source PPC terms found for Aura, TurboTax, LegalZoom (official), Sucuri, accessiBe, UserWay, Northwest. Program terms on Impact/CJ/PartnerStack are behind logins.
- Hotmart's general platform-level Terms of Use text on Google Ads could not be retrieved; only blog guidance.

## (A3) Realistic affiliate PPC economics and Google's affiliate/bridge-page policies

### Takeaway
Google disallows bridge/doorway pages that only forward traffic, and it limits one ad per auction location for the same business. Affiliates therefore need original landing pages and still often lose the auction to the merchant. Platform-wide EPC data (ClickBank: $0.80 EPC from Google Ads) is below typical CPCs in high-value pain niches ($5-9), so pure affiliate search arbitrage is structurally hard. Profitable cases found are anecdotal and small.

### Cited Findings
- Google "Insufficient original content" policy: "bridge pages, doorways, gateways, or other intermediate pages that are only used to link to other sites" are not allowed as ad destinations; violations do not lead to immediate suspension without warning; fix by adding useful original content (official page, via search extract) — [Google: Insufficient original content](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16427718)
- Unfair Advantage: prohibits showing more than one ad at a time for the same business/site. An April 2025 clarification says this applies to a "single ad location", and the live policy wording vs announcement wording is inconsistent. It also lists promoting the same content from multiple accounts on similar queries — [Google policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6020954?hl=en); [WordStream on update](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/google-ads-unfair-advantage-update); [Blushark Digital](https://blusharkdigital.com/blog/google-ads-policy-update-unfair-advantage-policy-changes/)
- Hotmart + Google Ads: Brazilian affiliate blogs report Hotmart obtained Google certification in 2025, reopening the ability to point ads directly at the producer's URL with the HotLink in the tracking template. Because Google shows one ad per query for the same sales page, competing affiliates crowd each other out. Sources also say using the producer's page as-is can be flagged as duplicate content (third-party, ANECDOTAL/unverified) — [GPA Domínio Estratégico](https://gpadominioestrategico.com.br/link-de-afiliado-da-hotmart-no-google-ads-como-usar-corretamente/); [Empreendedor Lifestyle](https://empreendedorlifestyle.com.br/link-de-afiliado-hotmart-no-google-ads-2025/); [Álvaro Mendonça](https://alvaromendonca.com/anunciar-sem-site-no-google-ads/)
- ClickBank direct-linking: ClickBank's own guide says you can link to the vendor page but a landing page is usually better; other sources say Google doesn't allow direct linking (conflicting) — [ClickBank guide](https://www.clickbank.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-with-google-ads/)
- ClickBank Google Ads averages: 1.34% hop conversion rate, $0.80 EPC, $67.19 average payout — [ClickBank](https://www.clickbank.com/blog/best-traffic-sources-for-affiliate-marketing)
- ANECDOTAL: one ClickBank + Google Ads case study reports ~$250 revenue on ~CA$67 spend early on; the author warns this is unusual at the start. A BlackHatWorld post reports "+$700" in a saturated niche without full costs — [Ivan Mana case study](https://ivanmana.com/clickbank-google-ads-case-study-how-to-build-a-profitable-campaign-everything-revealed/); [BlackHatWorld](https://www.blackhatworld.com/seo/making-700-with-google-ads-in-a-super-saturated-niche-guide-and-case-study.1683253/)
- NordVPN/Northwest terms reverse commissions on refunds/cancellations (90 days for Northwest), which adds clawback risk to PPC math — [Awin NordVPN](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/15132); [Awin Northwest](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/66946)

### Inferences
- Break-even math (illustrative, not sourced data): at a $5.26 average CPC (LocaliQ 2025 all-industry), a $100 commission needs roughly a 5.3% click-to-sale rate just to break even. At ClickBank's 1.34% hop conversion, break-even CPC for a $67 payout is about $0.90, far below US CPCs in legal, finance and home-services niches. Pure affiliate on Google Search likely only works in long-tail, low-CPC queries or in countries/languages with lower CPCs (e.g., Brazil).
- Owning the offer (own product or lead-gen with direct buyer contracts) keeps 100% of margin, lets you bid on your own brand, and avoids bridge-page and unfair-advantage conflicts.

### Gaps
- No rigorous, non-anecdotal public dataset found on affiliate search arbitrage profitability by niche.
- No network-reported EPCs for the specific urgent-pain programs (Northwest, Aura, NordVPN) were found; Impact/CJ show EPC only to logged-in publishers.

## (B1) Restricted/certified Google Ads categories relevant to pain niches

### Takeaway
Many of the strongest "urgent pain" niches are restricted. Consumer tech support (including data recovery, virus removal, account recovery) is banned for third parties. Credit repair is banned. Debt services need certification in 11 countries. Locksmith and garage-door ads need Advanced Verification in the US/Canada. Government-document services get a forced "Not a government website" label, and since Oct 2026 only explicitly government-authorized providers can be certified. Financial services verification is expanding across the EU (July 2026) and to US/Canada licences (2027).

### Cited Findings
**Tech support (data recovery, malware removal, account recovery)**
- Google prohibits ads for third-party technical support for consumer technology products and online services. Examples include data recovery, security, virus removal, booting, internet connectivity, account recovery, hardware repair and software installation. Exemptions: sale of consumer technology itself, and tech support offered exclusively to businesses. A warning comes at least 7 days before suspension (official page, via search extract). In force since 2019 — [Google: Third-party consumer technical support](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/13527027); [Google update notice](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9479396?hl=en); [ITNews 2019](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/google-to-nix-all-tech-support-provider-ads-511909)
- A 2025 podcast describes an October 2025 update distinguishing B2C vs B2B; details not verified — [podcast](https://alanawu.podbean.com/e/does-google-ads-allow-tech-support-ads-policy-updated-october-2025)

**Government documents and official services**
- Feb 2025: certification no longer required for vignettes/e-vignettes across Europe; narrower exemption for France-only business-identifier ads; enforced from Feb 5, 2025 — [Google policy update Feb 2025](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15751624)
- From Oct 22, 2025: Google auto-adds a "Not a government website" disclosure to Search ads in this category unless the advertiser is certified as a government provider, and advertisers cannot remove it — [Google policy update Sept 2025](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16561193?hl=en); [Kliken help](https://help.kliken.com/en/articles/12461228-google-ads-policy-update-government-documents-and-services-disclosure-october-2025)
- Enforcement from Oct 5, 2026: to qualify as authorized, an advertiser's domain must be linked from an official government website and explicitly referenced as authorized for that specific document/service. A general association with government processes is not enough. E-visas and ETAs are explicitly covered (trade press; confirm on Google's page) — [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/google-ads-government-documents-services-policy-update-october-2026); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-tightens-ads-policy-for-government-document-providers-484342); [VisasNews](https://visasnews.com/en/google-tightens-advertising-rules-for-visas-and-etas/)

**Local services (locksmith, garage door)**
- US ads for locksmith and garage-door repair require Advanced Verification; locksmith also in Canada. Plumbers are listed only for the Netherlands (ads not allowed). HVAC is not mentioned in results (official page, via search extract) — [Google: Local services](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/13527633); [Google Advanced Verification](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/7167922?hl=en-AU); [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/search-marketing-listings-management/google-advanced-verification-locksmiths-and-garage-door/)
- Third-party: verification checks business registration/licences plus a recorded owner video interview, renewed ~every 12 months — [Enrich Labs 2026](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/garage-door-marketing-complete-guide-2026)

**Debt services / credit repair**
- Debt settlement/debt management ads (including lead generators) are allowed only in Australia, Brazil, Canada, Germany, Ireland, Japan, South Africa, South Korea, Spain, UK and US, and only with Google certification (official page, via search extract) — [Google: Debt services](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9520029?hl=en-GB)
- US: only approved nonprofit budget and credit counseling agencies (11 U.S.C. §111) may advertise debt services (secondary source) — [LaunchGuarding](https://www.launchguarding.com/blog/google-debt-services-ads)
- June 2, 2025: debt-services verification in Australia, Brazil, Germany, Ireland, South Korea and Spain moved into the Financial Services verification program; already-certified advertisers need not re-verify — [Google update June 2025](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16292878?hl=en); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-streamlines-debt-services-verification-in-six-countries/)
- Credit repair ads banned since 2019 — [WordStream 2019](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2019/10/30/google-restricts-financial-services-ads); [Consumer Finance Monitor](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2019/12/09/google-updates-financial-products-and-services-policy-to-restrict-advertising-of-debt-relief-services/)

**Financial services verification**
- UK has had its own Financial Services Verification since Sept 2021 — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-expands-financial-ad-verification-to-24-eu-and-eea-countries/)
- 2026: expansion to 24 more EEA countries (Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Sweden), enforcement from July 23, 2026; ~42 countries total; 30 days to verify, then ads restricted — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-expands-financial-services-ad-verification-across-24-european-markets-480833); [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/eu-financial-advertiser-verification/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-expands-financial-ad-verification-to-24-eu-and-eea-countries/)
- US and Canadian financial advertisers to be required to prove licences in 2027 (trade press headline) — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-forces-us-and-canadian-financial-advertisers-to-prove-licences-in-2027/)
- Advertisers in certain financial categories must show they're licensed by the relevant regulator, or exempt, in each targeted location — [Google: Tasks for advertiser verification](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15577076?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Viable for an affiliate/low-touch owner with little friction: website security for businesses (B2B tech support is exempt), LLC formation, ADA compliance tools, legal-document templates, tax/accounting software (software sale, not support), VPN/identity protection (product sale).
- Effectively blocked or high-friction: consumer data recovery services / "fix my computer" / account recovery (prohibited for third parties; selling data-recovery *software* may fall under the "sale of consumer technology" exemption but is a gray area), credit repair (banned), debt relief (certification + nonprofit requirement in the US), locksmith/garage door (owner video verification + licence), government-document "help" services (forced disclaimer, near-impossible certification after Oct 2026), anything financial in UK/EU/BR without regulator licence.

### Gaps
- Could not verify a general Google "legal services" certification. Google does not appear to have a global one; country-specific rules (e.g., bail bonds prohibition, some EU national restrictions) were not retrieved.
- Health/pharma/addiction-treatment (LegitScript) certification rules were not researched within budget.
- "Consumer recovery services" (fund/asset recovery) Google policy text was not retrieved.
- HVAC: no evidence of Advanced Verification requirement found; Google Local Services Ads (separate product) have their own screening.

## (B2) Advertiser verification for a foreign (e.g., Brazilian) advertiser targeting US/EU

### Takeaway
Google's Advertiser verification is tied to the payments profile country. Documents must match the payments-profile name and location exactly, the phone must carry the payments-profile country's area code, and the rep's photo ID can come from any country. A Brazilian individual or company can therefore verify with Brazilian documents and target the US/EU. Ads will show "paid for by" the verified name, and regulated categories need local licences in the targeted country regardless.

### Cited Findings
- Name and location on the payments profile must exactly match submitted documents; edit the profile name if needed (official page, via search extract) — [Google: Tasks required for Advertiser verification](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15577076?hl=en)
- Acceptable documents vary by country and account type (individual vs organization); contact Google before verifying if the account type is wrong — [Google: Document requirements](https://support.google.com:443/adspolicy/answer/9872280); [Google: Tasks](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15577076?hl=en)
- Phone number area code must be that of the payments profile country — [Google: Tasks](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15577076?hl=en)
- Authorized representative's government photo ID can be issued in any country (passport, state ID, driver's licence, green card) — [Google: Tasks](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15577076?hl=en)
- Ad disclosure shows "Ads funded by (payment profile name)" if verified name differs from payment profile name — [Google: Tasks](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15577076?hl=en)
- Financial-services categories require licence by the relevant regulator in the targeted location — [Google: Tasks](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15577076?hl=en)

### Inferences
- The foreign location itself is not a blocker for unregulated niches. For regulated niches (finance, debt, government docs, locksmith), a Brazilian entity generally cannot get the required US/UK/EU licences or certifications, which effectively excludes those niches.
- The advertiser name appears publicly in Google's Ads Transparency Center, so "no face" still means a named legal entity is visible.

### Gaps
- No source found specifically on foreign advertisers' extra scrutiny, or on whether Google requires local business presence for Advanced Verification (likely yes for locksmith/garage door since licences are local, but unverified).

## (B3) Benchmark CPCs by industry 2025/2026 (US; BR/EU where available)

### Takeaway
US Search CPCs rose in 2025. The LocaliQ/WordStream all-industry average is $5.26, though some coverage says $5.42. Legal is the most expensive at $8.58 CPC and ~$131.63 cost per lead in 2026 data. Finance & insurance and home improvement run $7+. No reliable Brazil or Europe industry-level Google Ads CPC benchmark was found. One Brazilian blog cites R$1-5 general search CPC (low-quality source).

### Cited Findings
- LocaliQ 2025 all-industry average search CPC $5.26 (from $4.66 in 2024); other coverage reports $5.42 with 87% of industries rising (conflict unresolved). CTR 6.66%; conversion rate 7.52% vs 8.18% depending on source — [LocaliQ search benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ads-costs-keep-rising-but-conversion-rates-improved-in-2025-477927); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-ads-costs-rise-12-88/)
- WordStream 2025 report: Attorneys & Legal Services $8.58 average CPC (highest). Overall cost per lead $70.11. Sample: 16,000+ campaigns, Apr 2024-Mar 2025 — [WordStream 2025 benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks); [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ads-costs-keep-rising-but-conversion-rates-improved-in-2025-477927)
- LocaliQ 2026 data: Attorneys & Legal Services cost per lead $131.63; 13,474 campaigns analysed — [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/search-advertising-benchmarks/); [Clique Studios summary](https://cliquestudios.com/university/resources/good-cost-per-lead-by-industry)
- Finance & insurance and home improvement consistently in the "$7+" CPC range; arts & entertainment and travel at $2-3 (secondary summary) — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/google-ads-cpcs-rose-to-5-42-in-2025-with-87-of-industries-seeing-increases-but-conversion-rates-climbed-to-8-18-as-automation-improves/)
- Older LocaliQ legal page: $9.21 legal CPC (Apr 2022-Mar 2023) — [LocaliQ legal benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/legal-search-advertising-benchmarks/)
- Brazil: blog cites Google Search CPC range R$1.00-5.00 in 2025, no methodology (low reliability) — [Sopa Cultural](https://sopacultural.com/a-inflacao-do-trafego-pago-no-brasil-por-que-anunciar-na-internet-esta-cada-vez-mais-caro/)
- Meta (not Google) Brazil median CPC ~$0.37 vs $1.13 global, which suggests BR auctions are cheaper in general (indirect evidence only) — [Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpc-cost-per-click/brazil)

### Inferences
- In the US, legal, finance and home-services pain niches carry $7-9+ average CPCs (individual hot keywords like "LLC formation" or "personal injury" are often much higher), so affiliate payouts under ~$100 are hard to make work.
- Brazil's lower CPCs (in BRL) improve the math for Hotmart/Kiwify info-products with R$100-500 tickets. The owner should validate with Keyword Planner rather than rely on the weak benchmark found.

### Gaps
- Full 2025 WordStream/LocaliQ industry table (exact business services, home services, finance & insurance CPCs and CPLs) could not be retrieved; pages were not fetchable from this environment.
- No credible Europe-specific (UK/DE/FR) Google Ads industry CPC benchmark found.
- No credible Brazil industry-level Google Ads CPC benchmark found. Keyword Planner data for specific keywords is recommended.
